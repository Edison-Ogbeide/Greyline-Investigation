How Engagement Works

A clear path to begin.

Every engagement begins with a confidential inquiry, submitted through our encrypted form, and proceeds to a written scope before any work is committed.

Step 01

Confidential inquiry

Use the encrypted intake form. Provide a high-level summary, no sensitive evidence required at this stage. An analyst, not a salesperson, reviews every submission.

Step 02

Confidential scoping call

Within two business days, we arrange a call to understand the matter, identify what is and isn't in scope, and explain our approach.

Step 03

Written scope & fixed fee

You receive a written engagement letter setting out the work, the deliverable, the timeline, and the fee. Nothing is committed until you sign.

Step 04

Engagement begins

Once the engagement letter is signed, work begins.
