FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions clients ask
before they instruct us.

The honest answers to what most people are quietly wondering. If your question isn't here, ask us directly — there is no obligation in doing so.

Is what you do legal?

Yes. Every Greyline investigation is conducted under UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, and ACPO Principles for Digital Evidence. We work exclusively with publicly available information, lawfully obtained records, and — where applicable — material disclosed with the informed consent of the subject. We do not hack accounts, intercept private communications, impersonate individuals, or pay for unlawfully obtained data. If a request would require us to cross those lines, we decline it.

Will the person I'm asking about find out?

In almost all cases, no. Our standard methodology is passive — we observe public information without interacting with the subject or their network. Where a service requires the subject's involvement (such as polygraph examinations or certain employment-screening services), their informed consent is a condition of the engagement and we say so clearly upfront. Discretion is the default. You will be told if anything about your specific case requires a different approach.

What does a Greyline report contain — and is it usable in court?

You receive a written PDF report covering scope, methodology, findings, supporting evidence (screenshots, archived records, source links), and a clear summary suitable for non-technical readers. For litigation-aligned engagements, reports are produced to evidential standards — chain-of-custody documentation, expert-witness statements where relevant, and bundles formatted for instructing solicitors or applications such as freezing orders and Norwich Pharmacal orders. We will tell you at scoping stage whether the work you need is intelligence-grade or evidence-grade. The two are priced and produced differently.

How long does an investigation take, and what does it cost?

Most personal-intelligence cases close within 7–14 working days. Litigation-aligned and cross-border investigations typically run 3–6 weeks. Continuous-monitoring programmes are ongoing. Fees are quoted in writing as fixed fees against a defined scope, never as open-ended hourly retainers — so you know what you are committing to before any work begins. Productised reports are priced per deliverable; complex or cross-jurisdictional engagements are scoped individually.

What if you investigate and find nothing?

A clean finding is still a finding — and often a valuable one. The absence of red flags, when established to a defensible standard, is what allows you to make a decision with confidence. Our quotes are based on the work conducted, not on a particular outcome being produced. We never inflate findings to justify a fee, and we will tell you in scoping if we believe the realistic outcome of an investigation is a confirmation rather than a revelation.

How do I know my information stays confidential?

Inquiries are handled under strict internal confidentiality protocols from first contact. A confidentiality agreement is provided before any detailed case discussion. Case records are kept on UK-jurisdiction infrastructure, encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is restricted to the assigned investigator. We do not discuss live or historical cases publicly, and we do not share, sell, or repurpose client data. Communication is by email and scheduled Google Meet only — no calls without prior arrangement.
