How Engagement Works

A clear path to begin.

Greyline operates a single, considered intake process. Every engagement begins with a confidential inquiry, submitted through our encrypted form and proceeds to a written scope before any work is committed.

Step 01

Submit a confidential inquiry

Use the encrypted intake form on our inquiry page. Provide a high-level summary of your situation, no sensitive evidence required at this stage. An analyst, not a salesperson, reviews every submission.

Step 02

Confidential scoping call

Within two business days, we arrange a 30-minute discovery call to understand the matter properly, identify what is and isn't within scope, and explain how we would approach the work.

Step 03

Written scope & quotation

You receive a clear written engagement letter setting out the work, the deliverables, the timeline, and the fee. Nothing is committed until you sign and return it.

Step 04

Engagement begins

Once the engagement letter is signed and the retainer received, work begins immediately.

Ready to begin

Submit a confidential inquiry.
