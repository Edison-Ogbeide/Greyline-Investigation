HOW WE WORK

A predictable, confidential process
from first contact to final report.

01

Confidential Inquiry

Submit a brief inquiry through our secure form or by email. No call-backs without your consent. No phone calls until we've corresponded by email.

02

Scoping & Quote

We respond within 1–2 business days with a written scoping note, fixed-fee quote, and a clear view of what we can — and cannot — achieve.

03

Engagement & Investigation

Once engaged, our investigators work to a defined deliverable. Updates are provided at agreed intervals — no calls without scheduling.

04

Final Report & Onward Action

You receive a written report and, where relevant, an evidence pack suitable for instructing a solicitor or referring to authorities.
