Engagement Process

How corporate engagements begin.

Productised services run on defined turnaround. Bespoke engagements are scoped against your specific exposure, jurisdictions, and operational requirements — with a written scope of work issued before any commitment.

Step One

Discovery

For bespoke engagements: a confidential 30-minute call to understand the context, confirm scope, identify jurisdictions, and surface any sensitivities.

Step Two

Scope & Engagement

A written scope of work and quotation is issued. On agreement, terms are signed and the engagement begins under defined deliverables and timelines.

Step Three

Investigation

A lead analyst owns the engagement end-to-end. Where field verification is required, vetted operatives are deployed under analyst supervision.

Step Four

Reporting

You receive a structured intelligence report — risk-rated, evidenced, and ready to inform commercial decisions, regulatory filings, or onward legal action.

Volume engagements & retainer arrangements

Pre-Hire Intelligence Reports are available at discounted unit pricing for clients commissioning five or more reports per month. Vendor & Supply Chain Risk Reports are available under volume contracts for clients onboarding ten or more vendors annually. For ongoing intelligence partnerships across multiple matters, retainer arrangements with dedicated analyst access are available on request.
