Greyline Pre-Hire Intelligence

Pre-employment vetting that goes meaningfully beyond compliance-tier background checks. Standard tier covers identity, right-to-work, criminal record, employment and education verification, reference authentication, and a digital footprint review. Executive tier adds extended criminal checks, directorship and litigation searches, the digital behaviour intelligence report, PEP and sanctions screening, and source-country field verification for foreign hires.

Standard £499 · Executive £1,749
  • Identity and right-to-work verification
  • Criminal record check (Basic / Standard / Enhanced as applicable)
  • Employment and education verification through documented sources
  • Reference authentication — confirming the references actually exist and worked there
  • Digital footprint and adverse media review
  • Executive tier: directorship and litigation searches, PEP and sanctions screening, source-country field verification
Suited for: regulated firms, businesses making senior or trust-critical hires, founders making early-stage hires where role exposure is high, family offices employing household and personal staff, and any organisation where a bad hire costs more than the vetting ever will.

Vendor & Supply Chain Risk Assessment

Structured intelligence on a vendor, contractor, or supply-chain counter-party before contract signature, renewal, or capital commitment. We map the corporate structure and beneficial ownership, screen for sanctions and adverse media exposure, assess litigation history, evaluate operational and reputational risk, and flag the matters that compliance-tier vendor due diligence routinely misses.

Quoted post-inquiry
Suited for: businesses onboarding suppliers in higher-risk jurisdictions, procurement teams preparing for contract renewal, organisations bound by supply-chain compliance regulations (Modern Slavery Act, sanctions regimes), and any business committing material spend to a counter-party they don't fully know.

Insider Threat Investigations

Investigation into suspected insider activity — IP exfiltration, fraudulent expense behaviour, conflict-of-interest breaches, undisclosed competing employment, leaks to the press or to competitors, and breach of restrictive covenants by departing employees. Conducted with the discretion and evidential rigour required to support a disciplinary process, civil action, or criminal referral.

Quoted post-inquiry
Suited for: businesses suspecting an insider threat in progress, organisations dealing with the aftermath of a confirmed leak, employers concerned about a departing employee's conduct, and any matter where suspicion needs to be either confirmed or eliminated to evidential standard.
