The Question This Answers

"Before I marry them, sponsor them, or build a life with them — who is this person, really?"

Marriage is a legal contract. Sponsorship binds you financially to another person, often across borders, often for years. Relocation rearranges your entire life around a relationship. These are decisions that cannot be reversed without significant cost — emotional, financial, and legal.

Most clients who commission Pre-Commitment Vetting are not in doubt. They have already decided. What they need is not validation; it is the confidence that comes from having looked properly, before the commitment is made. Some find what they hoped to find — and proceed with conviction. Others find what they did not expect — and avoid a mistake that would have shaped the rest of their lives.

What's Included

Three components, combined into one report.

Pre-Commitment Vetting brings together the depth of our Tier 3 Footprint Analysis with comprehensive criminal and background checks and full identity verification. Each component is conducted to evidential standard; the combined report gives you a complete picture of the person you're about to commit to.

  • Component 01 — Tier 3 Digital Behaviour Intelligence ReportComplete digital footprint including hidden accounts, intimate platforms, behavioural profile, and analyst-led interpretation of what the evidence means.
  • Component 02 — Criminal & Background ChecksUK criminal record check (DBS Basic or Standard where applicable), country-of-origin equivalent, civil litigation history, bankruptcy and insolvency, and adverse media screening.
  • Component 03 — Identity VerificationDocument authentication of passport or national ID, biometric confirmation, address and identity history, and verification that the person you're committing to is who they claim to be.
  • Marriage & Relationship Status VerificationConfirmation that the subject is legally free to marry — no current spouse, no undisclosed prior marriages, no pending divorce concealed from you.
  • Sanctions, PEP & Watchlist ScreeningCross-checking against UK, EU, and international sanctions lists, politically exposed persons databases, and law enforcement watchlists.
  • Employment & Education VerificationConfirmation that the employment, qualifications, and career history the subject has represented to you are accurate.
  • Financial Profile IndicatorsSurface-level financial stability indicators — undisclosed debt, lifestyle inconsistent with declared income, bankruptcy history.
  • Comprehensive Combined ReportA single integrated report bringing all three components together — with executive summary, structured findings, supporting evidence, and a clear recommendation framework.

A note on country-of-origin field verification

For non-UK subjects or foreign nationals, the standard Pre-Commitment Vetting Report includes country-of-origin criminal record checks where attainable through official channels. Where in-country physical verification is required — for example, confirming the subject's family, address, employer, or prior relationships exist as represented — this is quoted separately as an add-on (typically £500–£1,500 depending on country and scope), and can be commissioned at the discovery stage or after the initial report.

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions clients ask before instructing.

Is the subject's consent required?

For the digital footprint and OSINT components, no — these use lawful sources of information that do not require subject consent. For certain background-check elements (formal DBS checks, some international criminal record checks, employment verification through official channels), the subject's cooperation is required and they will know they are being verified.

In practice, most clients commissioning Pre-Commitment Vetting in a sponsorship or marriage context have a legitimate basis to ask for the subject's cooperation — and many subjects, if they have nothing to hide, are willing to participate. The lead analyst will discuss the specifics with you at the scoping call and design the engagement accordingly.

What if the subject refuses to cooperate with elements requiring consent?

That is itself a finding. The lead analyst will report it clearly in the executive summary, alongside the components that were completed without subject cooperation (which still produce substantial intelligence). A subject's refusal to participate in legitimate verification before a major commitment is meaningful information.

Is this lawful?

Yes. Greyline operates strictly within the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and GDPR. Each component of the engagement uses lawful sources and methods. Where any component requires the subject's consent (e.g., formal DBS or international criminal record checks), that consent is obtained through proper channels before the check is run.

How does this differ from Footprint Analysis Tier 3?

Footprint Analysis Tier 3 is the digital component on its own. Pre-Commitment Vetting includes the Tier 3 Footprint and adds two further components: comprehensive criminal and background checks (UK and country-of-origin) and full identity verification. It is designed for the irreversible-decision context — marriage, sponsorship, relocation — where digital intelligence alone is not enough.

If your situation does not warrant the full vetting product, Footprint Analysis Tier 3 (£1,699) may be more appropriate. If you're not sure, an analyst will advise.

What if the subject is in another country?

Pre-Commitment Vetting is regularly conducted on subjects in other countries — it is one of the most common use cases. Country-of-origin checks are included in the standard scope where attainable through official channels. Where in-country field verification (physical confirmation of address, employer, family) is required, that is added as a quoted supplement at the scoping stage.

Common countries for cross-border vetting include Nigeria, Ghana, Philippines, India, Pakistan, Ukraine, Russia, Brazil, Colombia, and various Eastern European countries. Other jurisdictions are accommodated; the analyst will advise on what is achievable for your specific case.

What if you find something serious?

The report tells you, plainly and with supporting evidence. The 30-minute analyst briefing call — included in every engagement — is designed precisely for cases where the findings warrant careful discussion. The analyst will walk you through what was found, what it means, what further investigation might clarify it, and how to think about your decision.

What we do not do is deliver findings without context, or leave you to interpret a 50-page report alone. Pre-Commitment Vetting is built around the assumption that you may need to think carefully about what you've learned.

Will the subject know I commissioned this?

For the digital and OSINT components, no — they are entirely untraceable. For components requiring subject consent (formal background checks), the subject will know that verification is being conducted, but they will not know who commissioned it unless you choose to tell them. In a sponsorship or marriage context, most clients are open about the fact of the verification — it is normal due diligence, not a covert investigation.

How long does the report remain valid?

The findings reflect the date of investigation. Most components remain meaningfully valid for 6–12 months; criminal record components for shorter periods due to ongoing changes. If a significant period passes between report delivery and your commitment, a refresh engagement (priced at a discount to the original) can be commissioned.

Can the report be used in a visa application?

The report is not a visa-application document — UK Visas & Immigration require their own documentation. However, the report can give you the confidence to proceed with the application, can identify areas requiring further documentation, and can flag inconsistencies before they become problems with a UKVI caseworker. Many clients commission Pre-Commitment Vetting precisely as the private check before lodging a sponsorship application.

How is my information handled?

All information is encrypted in transit and at rest, accessible only to the lead analyst and any specifically-authorised supporting personnel, and retained in line with our published Data Protection & Confidentiality Policy. We never share information about the engagement with anyone other than you, and we never disclose to the subject that an engagement has been commissioned (other than where their consent is required for specific components, as discussed above).
