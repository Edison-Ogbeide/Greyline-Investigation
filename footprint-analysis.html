What You Receive

The deliverable, by tier.

Each tier produces a distinct report. All are delivered as encrypted PDFs via secure link, structured for clarity, and written to be acted on rather than archived.

Three reports, one methodology.

Tier 1 is typically 8–12 pages — a structured map of the subject's identity and presence, organised by category. Tier 2 builds on that with a dedicated section on intimate-platform exposure, accompanied by screenshots and source references where lawfully captured. Tier 3 is typically 20–35 pages and includes the analyst's interpretive judgement on the totality of the digital evidence — what it suggests, what is consistent with normal life, and what isn't.

Every report is delivered via encrypted secure link to your nominated email. Working files are destroyed after delivery unless retention supports an active onward engagement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Things people need to know before instructing.

How is this different from Greyline Verdict?

Greyline Verdict is for an early-stage situation — typically someone you've met online and want to verify before progressing. The deliverable is a single Green / Amber / Red verdict.

Footprint Analysis is for an existing relationship where you want to investigate your partner's digital life in depth. It is investigative rather than verificatory — the question is not "are they real" but "what are they hiding".

Will my partner find out they have been investigated?

No. The work is conducted entirely from open and lawful sources, leaves no trace with the subject, and is delivered to you confidentially. We do not contact, message, or interact with the subject at any point.

Is this lawful in the UK?

Yes. We operate under the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and GDPR (UK), on confirmed lawful basis — the legitimate interests of a client investigating someone they are personally engaging with. We do not access private accounts, intercept communications, hack devices, or use any technique that crosses lawful lines.

If at any point the engagement would require techniques outside what UK law permits, we say so and decline that element of the work.

Which tier should I choose?

If your concern is "what accounts and contact details exist that I don't know about" — Tier 1.

If your concern is "are they on dating apps or adult platforms" — Tier 2.

If your concern is "what is the totality of who this person is online, and what does it mean" — or you're considering a major commitment — Tier 3.

If you're unsure, instruct Tier 2. It covers the most common concerns and you can upgrade to Tier 3 with credit applied if the findings warrant it.

What information do I need to provide?

At minimum: full name, date of birth (or approximate age), and at least one phone number or email address. The more identifiers you can share — known accounts, photos, the city they live in, employment details, anything you already suspect — the more precise the work.

After you instruct, we send a secure intake form. We don't expect you to know what's relevant — the form asks the right questions.

What if you find something serious?

The report sets out findings clearly, with evidence, regardless of how difficult they may be. We do not soften findings to spare the client; the value of the report depends on its honesty.

The report includes a recommended next-steps section. Where findings indicate criminality (for example, contact with vulnerable persons), we may include a recommendation to involve appropriate authorities.

Can the report be used in divorce or family proceedings?

The Tier 3 report is frequently used by solicitors as supporting intelligence in divorce, financial remedy, and custody proceedings. For matters where the report is to be formally introduced as evidence, we recommend additional engagement under our Litigation Support service — where the chain-of-custody, methodology documentation, and evidential standards required by court are built into the engagement.

How long does the work take?

Tier 1: typically 5–7 working days. Tier 2: typically 7–10 working days. Tier 3: typically 10–14 working days. Where urgency is required, expedited delivery is available on request — quoted at the point of instruction.
