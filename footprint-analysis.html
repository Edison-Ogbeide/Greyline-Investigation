The Question This Answers

"I know there's something I'm not seeing. I just don't know what — or where to look."

The doubts in an existing relationship rarely arrive as a single clear suspicion. They build — a phone held differently, an unexplained absence, a name that comes up too casually, a pattern of distraction that has no innocent explanation. Most partners describe the same thing: they didn't need someone to convince them something was wrong. They needed someone to confirm it, or to put it to rest.

Footprint Analysis is built for that situation. Three tiers, structured by depth, designed to give you a complete digital picture of your partner's online life — the accounts you know about and the ones you don't.

How It Works

Step Two

Who This Is For

For partners with specific concerns.

Footprint Analysis is built for situations where a relationship already exists and there is a specific reason to investigate. It is not appropriate for general curiosity, surveillance of a former partner, or any scenario without a defensible interest.

  • You suspect your partner is using dating apps, maintaining secret accounts, or conducting another relationship online.
  • You've discovered a phone number, email, or account you didn't know existed and want to know what's tied to it.
  • Your partner's behaviour has changed in ways that don't have an innocent explanation, and you need to confirm or rule out a digital cause.
  • You're considering ending the relationship and want clarity on what's been happening before you make the decision.
  • You're preparing for a difficult conversation with your partner and want to enter it with facts rather than suspicions.
  • You suspect adult content consumption, OnlyFans subscriptions, or other online activity inconsistent with the relationship.
Frequently Asked Questions

The questions clients actually ask.

Will my partner know they've been investigated?

No. Footprint Analysis is conducted entirely through open-source intelligence, authorised data sources, and the information you provide as the instructing client. The subject receives no notification, no contact, and no indication that any investigation is underway. Our methodology is designed to leave no trace.

Is this lawful?

Yes — within strict limits. Greyline operates within the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and GDPR. We use lawful sources of information only: open-source intelligence, public records, breach data within the limits of lawful access, and information you as the client provide. We do not access private accounts, intercept communications, or engage in any unlawful means.

If you suspect physical surveillance is required, that requires a different engagement (Field Relationship Investigation) with stricter scoping and lawful basis assessment.

Which tier should I choose?

Tier 1 if your concern is hidden communication channels — secret phone numbers, separate email accounts, alias social media. It maps the full surface of identity.

Tier 2 if your concern is hidden romantic or sexual activity — dating apps, adult platforms, secret accounts that exist for that purpose. Most relationship-investigation clients land here.

Tier 3 if you need to understand the whole picture — patterns, behaviour, character signals, and an analyst's interpretation of what the digital evidence actually means. Often used by clients preparing for major decisions (divorce proceedings, relocation, sponsorship).

If you're not sure, submit a confidential inquiry — an analyst will recommend the right tier based on your situation.

What if you don't find anything?

The report tells you that — clearly. A "clean" Footprint Analysis is a meaningful finding, not a failure. Many clients commission Footprint Analysis precisely to confirm that nothing is wrong, and a thorough investigation that finds no concerns has its own value.

What we don't do is invent findings to justify the engagement, or stretch ambiguous signals into accusations. The report reflects what was actually found.

Can the report be used in divorce proceedings?

Footprint Analysis reports are produced to evidential standards and are routinely used by clients to brief their solicitors. They are not, in themselves, court documents — but they document findings in a way that supports onward legal action.

If you are actively in or preparing for divorce proceedings and need work specifically structured for use in family court, you may want to combine Footprint Analysis with our Matrimonial & Divorce Support engagement, which is designed with that context in mind from the outset.

Can you upgrade my tier after I've started?

Yes. If you start with Tier 1 or Tier 2 and decide you need deeper investigation, you can upgrade and pay only the difference. The work already completed is not repeated.

What if the subject lives in another country?

Footprint Analysis is conducted on subjects regardless of location. International subjects may take slightly longer, and certain countries with limited public records may produce shallower findings on Tier 1 specifically — we are transparent in the report about what was achievable.

How is my information handled?

All information is encrypted in transit and at rest, accessible only to the analyst assigned to your case, and retained in line with our published Data Protection & Confidentiality Policy. We never share information about the engagement with anyone other than you, and we never disclose to the subject that an engagement has taken place.
