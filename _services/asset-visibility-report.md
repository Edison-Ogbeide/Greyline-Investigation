title: "Asset Visibility Report (Pre-Litigation)"
slug: asset-visibility-report
description: "A snapshot of a named individual or company's visible assets, property, and business interests, before proceedings begin or a dispute escalates."
problem_headline: "You cannot plan for a dispute if you do not know what the other side actually has."
problem_statement: >-
  Family disputes moving toward divorce proceedings, commercial
  disagreements heading toward litigation, and disputes with individuals
  suspected of dissipating assets all share the same early question:
  what does this person or company actually own, right now, before
  anything changes. Waiting until proceedings are issued to ask that
  question can mean asking it after assets have already moved. A
  pre-litigation snapshot, taken early and documented properly, gives a
  legal team or a family a factual starting point rather than a guess,
  and can materially change the strategy adopted from the outset.
deliverable: >-
  A structured report on the visible assets connected to a named
  individual or company, covering property, business interests,
  directorships, and any early indicators of insolvency or asset
  transfer, delivered with sourced findings suitable for a legal team
  or your own planning.
features:
  - "Property ownership, current and historical, across the UK"
  - "Company interests and directorships, current and dissolved"
  - "Early indicators of insolvency proceedings or financial distress"
  - "Recent asset transfers or disposals worth further investigation"
  - "A clear summary suitable for briefing a solicitor ahead of proceedings"
methodology: >-
  Property registry searches, corporate filing analysis, and court
  record checks for insolvency or dissipation indicators, built as a
  fast, factual snapshot rather than a full asset tracing exercise,
  with every finding verified by a named analyst before it is
  reported.
turnaround: "3 to 5 business days from confirmed scope, expedited review available where dissipation is a live concern."
faq:

    a: "This is a pre-litigation snapshot, taken before or early in a dispute. Asset Recovery Intelligence is the deeper, post-judgment tracing exercise once you have a judgment to enforce."
  - q: "Can this be updated if the matter runs on for months?"
    a: "Yes, through our Asset Visibility Monitoring subscription, which keeps this same picture current on a continuous basis for as long as the matter is live."
  - q: "Will the subject know this report has been commissioned?"
    a: "No. It is built entirely from publicly available information and lawfully obtained records, without contacting the subject."
