problem_headline: "Assets move. A one-off report is a snapshot of where they were."
problem_statement: >-
  An Asset Visibility Report answers where assets stood at the point the
  report was prepared. Where a matter is ongoing, a pending claim, a
  suspected pattern of dissipation, a family dispute moving toward
  litigation, a single snapshot is not enough. Assets can be moved,
  transferred, or restructured in the gap between reviews.
deliverable: >-
  Continuous monitoring for asset-related activity connected to a named
  subject, with a monthly intelligence briefing and priority alerts issued
  directly when a material change is confirmed.
features:
  - "Property acquisitions and disposals"
  - "New or dissolved company interests"
  - "Insolvency filings and court proceedings"
  - "Asset transfers between connected parties"
methodology: >-
  Continuous monitoring of property registries, corporate filings and court
  records, with every candidate finding verified by a named analyst before
  it is reported.
turnaround: "First briefing at the close of the first monitoring period; priority alerts issued within hours of a confirmed material finding."
faq:
  - q: "Is this useful before litigation has actually started?"
    a: "Yes. It is commonly used where dissipation is a live concern, ahead of or during pre-action correspondence, so a pattern of asset movement is documented as it happens rather than reconstructed afterward."
  - q: "How does this relate to the one-off Asset Visibility Report?"
    a: "The report is the initial pre-litigation snapshot. This subscription keeps that picture current where the matter is ongoing."
