title: "Insider Risk Monitoring"

description: "Continuous, indicator-led monitoring for insider threat risk, so a developing pattern is visible before it becomes a confirmed incident."
problem_headline: "By the time an insider incident is investigated, the damage is already done."
problem_statement: >-
  An Insider Threat Intelligence Brief investigates a specific, already
  suspected incident. This subscription exists for organisations that want
  ongoing visibility into insider risk indicators generally, rather than
  waiting for a specific suspicion to justify an investigation.
deliverable: >-
  Continuous, indicator-led monitoring for developing insider risk within an
  agreed scope, with a monthly intelligence briefing and priority alerts
  issued directly when a material indicator is confirmed.
features:
  - "Documented risk indicators consistent with data exfiltration or unusual access patterns"
  - "Adverse media or public conduct inconsistent with role and seniority"
  - "Undisclosed competing business interests or employment"
  - "Signals consistent with a planned departure paired with heightened access activity"
methodology: >-
  Indicator-led monitoring combining behavioural analysis with available
  technical signals, agreed and scoped at the outset. Every candidate
  finding is verified by a named analyst before it is reported.
turnaround: "First briefing at the close of the first monitoring period; priority alerts issued within hours of a confirmed material finding."
faq:
  - q: "Is this the same as monitoring our IT systems for data loss?"
    a: "It complements rather than replaces a technical DLP solution. Where the monitoring scope includes technical signals, that is agreed and integrated at the scoping stage; the intelligence layer is what interprets the pattern."
  - q: "What happens once a material indicator is confirmed?"
    a: "You receive a priority alert with the documented basis for the finding, so you can decide on next steps, which may include escalating to a full Insider Threat Intelligence Brief."
