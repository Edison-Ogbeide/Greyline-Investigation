description: "Our most comprehensive pre-commitment investigation, for the moment before marriage, cohabitation, joint property purchase, or relocation."
problem_headline: "Before you marry, cohabit, or commit financially, who are they, really?"
problem_statement: >-
  Built for the moment before a major life decision, marriage, cohabitation,
  joint property purchase, business partnership with a romantic partner,
  sponsorship of a partner's visa, or relocation to be with someone. These
  are decisions that, if wrong, are catastrophic financially, emotionally
  and legally, and the cost of the investigation is a fraction of the cost
  of being wrong.
deliverable: >-
  An annotated evidence report with every finding sourced, dated and
  cross-referenced, prepared in a format suitable for civil proceedings
  where relevant, divorce petitions, prenuptial disputes, or financial
  remedy applications, concluded with a debrief call with the lead analyst.
features:
  - "Full digital footprint sweep: social media, dating and lifestyle platforms, reverse image search, and forum/public mention checks across 300+ platforms"
  - "Behavioural intelligence: a Digital Behaviour Intelligence Report covering persona analysis, authenticity assessment and consistency scoring"
  - "Identity and document verification: marriage/divorce record checks, document authentication, and professional licence verification"
  - "Criminal and legal record checks: UK and country-of-origin equivalents, sex offender registers, and adverse media screening"
  - "Physical field verification where required: family and associate verification, source-country checks, and neighbourhood enquiries via Greyline's in-country network"
methodology: >-
  Runs across five layers, digital footprint, behavioural intelligence,
  identity and document verification, criminal and legal record checks,
  and, where required, physical field verification in the subject's country
  of origin, each cross-referenced against the others before the report is
  finalised.
turnaround: "10 to 14 business days, concluded with a debrief call with the lead analyst. Field-verification timelines are confirmed at intake."
faq:
  - q: "Does this include in-person checks abroad?"
    a: "Where required, yes, physical field verification is conducted through Greyline's network of trusted in-country investigators, including family/associate verification and local neighbourhood enquiries. Timelines and scope for field work are confirmed at intake."
  - q: "Is the report usable in court?"
    a: "It's prepared to a format suitable for civil proceedings, every finding sourced and dated, for matters such as prenuptial disputes or financial remedy applications. Whether it's sufficient for a specific proceeding is confirmed with you and, where relevant, your solicitor at scoping."
  - q: "How is this different from Relationship Authenticity Verification?"
    a: "Relationship Authenticity Verification answers a narrower question: is this person and the relationship what they appear to be. This assessment is built for the moment before a major, hard-to-reverse decision and runs five layers deep, digital, behavioural, identity and document, criminal and legal, and, where required, physical field verification, rather than a single focused check."
  - q: "Do we need a specific reason to commission this, or is general due diligence enough?"
    a: "General due diligence ahead of marriage, cohabitation, or a significant financial commitment is a common and entirely reasonable reason on its own. You do not need a specific suspicion to want a clear, factual picture before a decision that is difficult to reverse."
  - q: "Will the subject know this has been commissioned?"
    a: "The desk-based layers are built entirely from publicly available information and lawfully obtained records, without contacting the subject. Physical field verification, where instructed, is conducted discreetly through vetted in-country investigators rather than direct contact, unless direct verification is specifically agreed as part of your scope."
  - q: "What happens on the debrief call?"
    a: "The lead analyst walks you through every finding, what was verified, what could not be verified, and what any inconsistencies actually mean in practical terms, so you leave with a clear picture rather than just a written report to interpret alone."
