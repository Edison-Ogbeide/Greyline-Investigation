problem_statement: >-
  Unauthorised AI agents and automated bot activity are a standing risk
  category that a one-off assessment only captures at a single point in
  time. New agents get connected, existing ones gain access they were not
  intended to have, and automated activity against your organisation
  evolves. This subscription provides ongoing visibility rather than a
  snapshot.

  Continuous monitoring across the disciplines below, delivered as a monthly
  intelligence briefing plus priority alerts issued directly when a material
  finding is confirmed.
features:
  - "Unauthorised AI agents identified within monitored systems or integrations"
  - "Autonomous bot activity directed at your organisation, its platforms, or its customers"
methodology: >-
  Continuous behavioural monitoring to distinguish human from automated or
  AI-driven activity within the agreed scope, with every candidate finding
  verified by a named analyst. Findings are worded as consistent with, not
  proof of, automation.
turnaround: "First briefing at the close of the first monitoring period; priority alerts issued within hours of a confirmed material finding."
faq:
  - q: "Does this require direct access to our internal systems?"
    a: "Where monitoring extends to internal systems and integrations, access is agreed and scoped at the outset. Where the concern is external, customer-facing bot activity, no internal access is required."
  - q: "How do you tell a legitimate automated integration from an unauthorised one?"
    a: "Against an agreed baseline of known, sanctioned agents and integrations set at scoping. Anything operating outside that baseline is investigated and reported, with its access and activity documented."
  - q: "What happens once an unauthorised agent is confirmed?"
    a: "You receive a priority alert documenting what it had access to and for how long, so your technical team can assess impact and remediate without waiting for the next scheduled briefing."
