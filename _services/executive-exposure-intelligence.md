problem_statement: >-
  Individuals with a public profile, business leaders, public figures,
  high-net-worth individuals, carry a level of personal exposure that
  most people never have to think about: their movements, their
  family, their financial position, all more discoverable than they
  realise, and all potential material for impersonation, extortion, or
  targeted harassment. Understanding that exposure clearly is the first
  step to managing it.
deliverable: >-
  A structured personal exposure assessment for a high-profile
  individual, covering digital footprint, reputational exposure, and
  physical or family risk indicators visible from publicly available
  information, with practical recommendations.
features:
  - "Digital footprint and public exposure mapping"
  - "Reputational risk from adverse media and public commentary"
  - "Impersonation and doxxing exposure across web and social platforms"
  - "Physical and family exposure risk from publicly discoverable information"
  - "Practical, prioritised recommendations to reduce exposure"
methodology: >-
  Lawful open-source and dark web research into the individual's
  public footprint and known exposure, assessed against recognised
  targeting patterns relevant to high-profile individuals, with every
  finding verified by a named analyst before it is reported.
turnaround: "3 to 5 business days from confirmed scope."
