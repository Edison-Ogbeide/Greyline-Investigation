---
title: "Synthetic Narrative Intelligence"
slug: synthetic-narrative-intelligence
description: "Mapping a coordinated disinformation or reputation-attack campaign, including AI-generated content and bot amplification, and assessing how to respond."
problem_headline: "Something is being said about you, at scale, and it does not look organic."
problem_statement: >-
  A wave of negative reviews, comments, or articles has appeared faster and
  more uniformly than genuine, unconnected criticism usually does, and it is
  affecting how your organisation is perceived. Coordinated narrative attacks
  increasingly combine AI-generated text with automated amplification, which
  makes them harder to distinguish from real, if unwelcome, public sentiment
  without structured analysis.
deliverable: >-
  An intelligence report mapping the narrative's spread: where it originated,
  how it has been amplified, whether the accounts involved show indicators of
  coordination or automation, and whether the content itself shows indicators
  consistent with AI generation. The report closes with response options
  appropriate to what was actually found.
features:
  - "Network and link analysis of the accounts amplifying the narrative"
  - "Content analysis for indicators consistent with AI-generated text"
  - "Timeline reconstruction of how the narrative spread and where it concentrated"
  - "A confidence-graded assessment of coordination, rather than an assumption of it"
  - "Practical response options, matched to the scale and nature of what was found"
methodology: >-
  Combines the network and link analysis discipline described on our method
  page with content authenticity analysis. Findings on AI involvement are
  worded as consistent with, not proof of, AI generation, and findings on
  coordination are graded by confidence rather than asserted outright.
turnaround: "5 to 10 business days, depending on the scale of the narrative and the number of platforms involved."
faq:
  - q: "Can you get the content taken down?"
    a: "Takedown is a platform decision, not ours. Where our findings support a takedown request, we prepare the documentation to support you or your legal team in making one."
  - q: "How do you tell coordinated activity from genuine criticism?"
    a: "Timing patterns, account creation clustering, content similarity, and amplification behaviour all leave a trace. We report what the trace actually shows, including where it does not support a finding of coordination."
  - q: "Is this only for public reputation attacks, or can it cover internal narratives too?"
    a: "The same disciplines apply to internal misinformation, for example a fabricated internal memo circulating among staff. Tell us the context at scoping."
