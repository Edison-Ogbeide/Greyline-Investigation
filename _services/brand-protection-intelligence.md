description: "Continuous monitoring for typosquatted domains, fake websites and brand impersonation used to defraud customers or damage reputation."
problem_headline: "Impersonation of your brand happens on someone else's timeline."
problem_statement: >-
  Fraudulent domains, fake websites and impersonation accounts are
  registered and taken down continuously, often faster than manual
  checks can keep pace with. By the time an impersonation is reported by
  a customer, a payment has often already been made to the wrong
  account, or a fake app has already been downloaded, and it may have
  been active for weeks before anyone at the organisation knew it
  existed.
deliverable: >-
  Continuous monitoring for domains, websites, social media accounts and
  mobile applications impersonating your brand, with a monthly
  intelligence briefing and priority alerts issued directly when a
  material impersonation is confirmed, and documentation prepared to a
  standard suitable for a takedown request wherever one is warranted.
features:
  - "Typosquatted and lookalike domain registrations"
  - "Fake websites impersonating your brand"
  - "Social media impersonation accounts"
  - "Fraudulent mobile applications and phishing campaigns"
  - "New impersonation infrastructure flagged as it is registered, not after it has been reported by a customer"
  - "Evidence prepared to a standard suitable for registrar, hosting and platform takedown requests"
methodology: >-
  Continuous domain registration monitoring, brand and trademark scanning
  across web and social platforms, and mobile app store monitoring, with
  every candidate finding verified by a named analyst before it is
  reported.
turnaround: "First briefing at the close of the first monitoring period; priority alerts issued within hours of a confirmed material impersonation."
faq:
  - q: "Can this support a takedown request?"
    a: "Yes. Findings are documented to a standard that supports takedown requests to registrars, hosting providers and platform trust and safety teams."
  - q: "Who typically instructs this?"
    a: "Brands with a large customer base exposed to phishing and impersonation fraud, and organisations that have already experienced a brand impersonation incident."
  - q: "Does this cover AI-generated impersonation, like deepfakes or synthetic executive video?"
    a: "This service focuses on infrastructure-level abuse: domains, websites, apps and accounts, regardless of how the content on them was produced. For AI-generated brand impersonation specifically, deepfakes, voice cloning and synthetic media, see our AI & Synthetic Intelligence capability's Executive & Brand Impersonation Monitoring, which the two are commonly instructed alongside."
  - q: "How is a typosquatted domain actually detected?"
    a: "Through continuous registration monitoring for domains that are visually or phonetically close to your own, cross-referenced against known typosquatting patterns, rather than waiting for a customer or employee to stumble across one and report it."
