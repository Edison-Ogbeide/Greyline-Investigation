---
title: "Executive Exposure Profile"
slug: executive-exposure-profile
description: "A point-in-time assessment of a named executive's digital footprint and exposure, before it is exploited by an attacker."
problem_headline: "Executives are targeted precisely because of what is publicly visible about them."
problem_statement: >-
  Senior executives carry a disproportionate share of an organisation's
  digital risk. Publicly available information about their role, their
  location, their family, and their communication patterns gives
  attackers exactly what they need to craft convincing impersonation
  attempts, business email compromise, or physical security threats.
  Most organisations have never systematically assessed what is
  actually visible about a given executive, or how it could be used
  against them or the organisation.
deliverable: >-
  A structured assessment of a named executive's digital footprint,
  covering public exposure, credential and data breach history, and
  impersonation risk, with practical recommendations to reduce
  exploitable exposure.
features:
  - "Public digital footprint mapping across web and social platforms"
  - "Credential and personal data exposure from prior breaches"
  - "Existing impersonation attempts or doxxing activity"
  - "Physical and family exposure risk from publicly available information"
  - "Practical, prioritised recommendations to reduce exposure"
methodology: >-
  Lawful open-source and dark web research into the named executive's
  public footprint and any prior data exposure, assessed against known
  targeting patterns used in executive-focused fraud and social
  engineering, with every finding verified by a named analyst before
  it is reported.
turnaround: "3 to 5 business days from confirmed scope."
faq:
  - q: "Is this only relevant to public-facing executives?"
    a: "No. Less publicly visible executives are sometimes more exposed than they realise, since they may never have considered what is discoverable about them, unlike executives who manage their public profile deliberately."
  - q: "What happens after the assessment, does Greyline fix the exposure?"
    a: "The report includes prioritised, practical recommendations. Some, like requesting data removal from a broker, can be actioned directly by the executive or their team; others may require coordination with your security function."
  - q: "Can this run continuously rather than as a one-off?"
    a: "Yes, through our Executive Exposure Monitoring subscription, which continues watching for new exposure after this initial assessment."
  - q: "Does this cover family members as well as the executive?"
    a: "Where family exposure is material to the executive's own risk, publicly available information connected to close family is included in the assessment, scoped with you in advance."
  - q: "How many executives can be assessed in one engagement?"
    a: "Scope is flexible, from a single named individual to an entire leadership team, and is confirmed on your scoping call based on which roles carry the most exposure."
