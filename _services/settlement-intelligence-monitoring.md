title: "Settlement Intelligence Monitoring"
slug: settlement-intelligence-monitoring
description: "Continuous monitoring of a counterparty's financial position and circumstances throughout negotiation, so settlement strategy reflects the current picture."
problem_headline: "Settlement leverage can shift while negotiations are ongoing."
problem_statement: >-
  Settlement Intelligence assesses a counterparty's financial position
  and settlement prospects at a fixed point in time. Negotiations that
  run over weeks or months can see a counterparty's position change,
  an asset disposal, a new business interest, a cross-border move, and
  those changes affect what a realistic settlement figure actually looks
  like.
deliverable: >-
  Continuous monitoring of a named counterparty's financial position and
  circumstances, with a monthly intelligence briefing and priority alerts
  issued directly when a material change is confirmed.
features:
  - "Changes to the counterparty's financial position"
  - "Asset disposals and new business interests"
  - "Cross-border activities affecting enforcement prospects"
  - "Developments that materially change settlement leverage"
methodology: >-
  Continuous monitoring of corporate, property and cross-border records
  relevant to the counterparty's financial position, with every candidate
  finding verified by a named analyst before it is reported.
turnaround: "First briefing at the close of the first monitoring period; priority alerts issued within hours of a confirmed material change."
faq:
  - q: "How does this differ from a one-off Settlement Intelligence assessment?"
    a: "The assessment is a single point-in-time picture of settlement prospects. This subscription keeps that picture current for as long as negotiations continue, since a counterparty's position can change materially during that time."
  - q: "Does this require a completed Settlement Intelligence assessment first?"
    a: "It is the usual starting point, since the initial assessment establishes the baseline financial picture that this subscription then monitors for change."
