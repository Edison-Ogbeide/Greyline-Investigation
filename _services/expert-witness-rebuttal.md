title: "Expert Witness Rebuttal"
slug: expert-witness-rebuttal
description: "An independent, evidential-standard rebuttal of an opposing expert's report on digital evidence, cyber, or OSINT findings."
problem_headline: "An opposing expert report does not become weaker just because you disagree with its conclusion."
problem_statement: >-
  When an opposing party serves an expert report on digital evidence,
  a cyber incident, or an open-source finding, the report needs to be
  tested on its actual substance: the methodology used, the assumptions
  made, the confidence level claimed against what the underlying data
  actually supports. A rebuttal built on general skepticism rather than
  a rigorous, independent re-examination of the same evidence carries
  little weight with a court and can weaken your position rather than
  strengthen it.
deliverable: >-
  An independent rebuttal report addressing the opposing expert's
  methodology, assumptions and conclusions directly, re-examining the
  underlying evidence where accessible, and prepared to the same CPR
  Part 35 procedural standard as the report it responds to.
features:
  - "Point-by-point analysis of the opposing report's methodology and assumptions"
  - "Independent re-examination of the underlying evidence where accessible"
  - "Clear identification of where confidence levels claimed exceed what the evidence supports"
  - "CPR Part 35 compliant drafting throughout"
  - "Prepared with the same evidential discipline applied across our own reports"
methodology: >-
  Independent re-analysis of the same underlying evidence and data the
  opposing report relies on, tested against our own methodology, with
  discrepancies and unsupported claims identified specifically rather
  than through general critique, and the rebuttal drafted to full CPR
  Part 35 compliance.
turnaround: "Scoped to the court timetable for service of rebuttal evidence; typically 2 to 3 weeks from confirmed instruction."
faq:
  - q: "Can you rebut a report even if you were not involved in the original evidence gathering?"
    a: "Yes. Where the underlying evidence or data is accessible, it can be independently re-examined, and the opposing report's methodology tested on its own terms regardless of who gathered the original evidence."
  - q: "What if the opposing report is actually sound?"
    a: "That is reported honestly. Our duty is to the accuracy of our own findings, not to producing a rebuttal that supports your position regardless of what the evidence shows."
  - q: "Does this cover the same disciplines as your CPR Part 35 Expert Report service?"
    a: "Yes, digital evidence, cyber incident analysis and OSINT findings, since rebuttal work requires the same underlying expertise as producing the original report."
  - q: "Can the expert give oral evidence responding to cross-examination on the rebuttal?"
    a: "Yes, this is anticipated from the outset, and the rebuttal is prepared with the same rigour needed to withstand cross-examination as any expert report."
  - q: "How quickly can this be turned around if the court deadline is tight?"
    a: "Expedited review is available where the service timetable is tight, discussed candidly on your scoping call so expectations are realistic from the outset."
