problem_headline: "A clean vetting result on day one does not stay current forever."
problem_statement: >-
  Candidate Risk Intelligence answers who someone was at the point of hire.
  For roles carrying sustained trust or exposure, senior finance, client
  facing, or safeguarding sensitive positions, organisations increasingly
  want to know if something material changes afterward: a new criminal
  charge, a directorship at a competing interest, or an adverse media
  event.
deliverable: >-
  Continuous monitoring of the individual's public record and digital
  footprint for the duration of the subscription, with a monthly briefing
  and priority alerts issued directly when a material change is confirmed.
features:
  - "New criminal or civil proceedings"
  - "Undisclosed directorships or business interests"
  - "Adverse media developments"
  - "Financial distress indicators (CCJs, insolvency)"
methodology: >-
  Continuous monitoring of public records, court listings and adverse media,
  with every candidate finding verified by a named analyst before it is
  reported.
turnaround: "First briefing at the close of the first monitoring period; priority alerts issued within hours of a confirmed material finding."
