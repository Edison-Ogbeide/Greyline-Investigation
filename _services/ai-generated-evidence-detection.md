problem_headline: "The other side's evidence does not sit right."
problem_statement: >-
  A document, recording, or image submitted by an opposing party in a dispute
  is being relied upon, and something about it warrants scrutiny before it
  goes unchallenged. As synthetic content becomes harder to spot by eye,
  legal teams increasingly need a technical assessment they can put in front
  of a court, not just a suspicion.
deliverable: >-
  A structured assessment of the disputed evidence's authenticity, prepared
  to a standard suitable for legal proceedings, with findings worded as
  consistent with, or not consistent with, AI generation or manipulation.
  Where instructed, this is accompanied by a formal rebuttal statement
  setting out the basis for challenging the evidence.
features:
  - "Technical authenticity analysis of the disputed document, recording or image"
  - "Cross-referencing against the case timeline and other evidence in the matter"
  - "Expert-witness-ready reporting, CPR Part 35 compliant formatting where the instruction calls for it"
  - "A formal rebuttal statement where instructed, setting out the basis for challenge"
  - "Chain-of-custody documentation for the disputed material as received"
methodology: >-
  Aligns with the evidential standard described in full on our Litigation
  Intelligence capability and our published method: original material is
  never altered, the audit trail is reproducible, and findings are worded to
  the standard of confidence the underlying analysis actually supports.
turnaround: "5 to 15 business days, depending on complexity and whether an expert witness statement is required."
