description: "A structured, discreet investigation into whether a relationship, and the person in it, is what it appears to be."
problem_headline: "A gut feeling that something is wrong deserves a proper answer, not just more worry."
problem_statement: >-
  Suspecting that a partner is not who they claim to be, whether met
  online or in person, is one of the most common and most difficult
  situations to act on alone. The most basic question is often the
  hardest to answer alone: is this person actually who they say they
  are, is their name, age, location and claimed background consistent
  with what independent sources show. Confronting someone directly
  without evidence rarely resolves anything and can damage a
  relationship that turns out to be genuine. Living with unresolved
  suspicion is its own cost. A discreet, evidence-based verification
  gives a factual answer either way, so a decision can be made on facts
  rather than anxiety.
deliverable: >-
  A structured verification of a relationship and the individual in
  it, built around a full identity consistency check, name, age,
  location, employment and other claimed details cross-referenced
  against independent sources, alongside claimed background and any
  indicators consistent with deception, delivered as a clear,
  documented finding.
features:
  - "Name, age, location and employment claims tested against publicly available and lawfully obtained records"
  - "Cross-platform check for other identities, profiles, or relationships"
  - "Photo provenance checks where images are part of the claimed identity"
  - "Consistency between what has been told to you and what is independently verifiable"
  - "Assessment against known romance fraud and deception patterns where relevant"
  - "A clear, documented finding, red flag, amber flag, or no significant indicators"
methodology: >-
  Lawful open-source research and public record verification, including
  a full identity consistency check and, where relevant, photo
  provenance analysis, cross-referenced against the details you have
  been given, with every finding sourced and every conclusion reviewed
  by a named analyst before it is reported.
turnaround: "3 to 5 business days from confirmed scope."
