description: "Forensic reconstruction of a Business Email Compromise incident where AI-generated messaging, voice, or impersonation played a part, with remediation recommendations."
problem_headline: "The invoice looked right. The money is gone."
problem_statement: >-
  A payment has gone to the wrong account, or a mailbox is showing signs of
  compromise, and the messaging involved reads more convincingly than the
  business email compromise attempts of a few years ago. Large language
  models have made fluent, context-aware phishing and impersonation emails
  routine, and a forensic response now has to account for that as a factor,
  not just look for the traditional tells.
deliverable: >-
  A forensic report reconstructing what happened: how access was obtained or
  what messaging was used, what controls were exploited or absent, whether
  the messaging or any associated audio shows indicators consistent with AI
  generation, and where the funds or data went where that can be traced.
  The report closes with specific, actionable remediation steps, not a
  generic security checklist.
features:
  - "Email header, forwarding-rule and mailbox audit"
  - "Timeline reconstruction of the compromise, from initial access to discovery"
  - "Linguistic analysis of suspicious messages for indicators consistent with AI-generated content"
  - "Financial-flow tracing where payments were misdirected, cross-referenced with our Fraud & Asset Intelligence capability where the matter extends into recovery"
  - "Chain-of-custody documentation for every artefact handled, to our published evidential standard"
methodology: >-
  Digital forensics conducted to ACPO Principles for Digital Evidence,
  combined with AI-content analysis on any suspect messaging or audio.
  Findings on AI involvement are worded as consistent with, not proof of,
  AI generation.
turnaround: "5 to 10 business days depending on the number of mailboxes, systems, and transactions in scope."
