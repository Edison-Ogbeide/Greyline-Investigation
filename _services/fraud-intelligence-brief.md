title: "Fraud Intelligence Brief"
slug: fraud-intelligence-brief
description: "An intelligence assessment of a suspected fraud, an investment scam, a romance-linked scheme, or general financial misrepresentation, before or after money has been sent."
problem_headline: "Fraud is rarely the work of a single opportunist. It is usually a professionally run operation."
problem_statement: >-
  Investment fraud, cryptocurrency scams, boiler room operations,
  romance-linked schemes, and fabricated financial positions are
  rarely the work of a single opportunist acting alone. These are
  typically organised operations running multiple identities, shell
  companies and platforms simultaneously, targeting numerous victims
  at once. A victim reporting to their bank or Action Fraud in
  isolation rarely gets the fuller picture of who is actually behind
  the scheme, what else it is connected to, or whether recovery is
  realistically possible.
deliverable: >-
  An intelligence brief on a suspected fraud, mapping the individuals,
  companies and platforms involved, cross-referencing them against
  known fraud patterns and prior reported schemes, and assessing
  realistic prospects for reporting or recovery action. Scope covers
  investment and cryptocurrency scams, romance-linked financial fraud,
  and general financial misrepresentation, whichever form the
  deception took.
features:
  - "Identity and corporate structure behind the scheme, platform, or individual"
  - "Connections to other reported scams or known fraud networks"
  - "Cryptocurrency wallets and payment routes, where funds were sent digitally"
  - "For romance-linked fraud, the financial pattern of the deception alongside the relationship itself"
  - "An honest assessment of realistic recovery prospects"
  - "A report suitable for Action Fraud, your bank's fraud team, or a solicitor"
methodology: >-
  Open-source and dark web research into the platform, individuals and
  companies involved, cross-referenced against known fraud typologies
  and prior reported schemes, combined with blockchain analysis where
  cryptocurrency was used, with every finding verified by a named
  analyst before it is reported.
turnaround: "48 to 72 hours from confirmed scope, given the time-sensitive nature of fraud response."
faq:
  - q: "Is it too late if I have already sent money?"
    a: "No. This brief is instructed both before money is sent, to verify a suspicious opportunity, and after, to understand what happened and support a recovery or reporting effort."
  - q: "Can this help me get my money back?"
    a: "It cannot guarantee recovery, but findings are structured to support your bank's fraud team, a police report, or, where cryptocurrency is involved, our Cryptocurrency Tracing & Recovery Intelligence service."
  - q: "Is this the same as your AI Romance Fraud Verification service?"
    a: "No. That service verifies whether a relationship and the person in it are authentic, typically before money has changed hands. This brief investigates a fraud where money has been sent or a financial ask made, romance-linked or otherwise, and focuses on who is behind it and what recovery looks like."
  - q: "What if this scheme has targeted other people too?"
    a: "Identifying that connection is a core part of this work. It can strengthen a police report and, in some cases, connect you with a wider pattern that increases the chance of a coordinated response."
  - q: "Will you tell me honestly if recovery looks unlikely?"
    a: "Yes. We report realistic prospects plainly rather than encouraging further spending on a recovery that the evidence does not support."
