title: "Source of Wealth Report"
slug: source-of-wealth-report
description: "Independent verification of a person's claimed source of wealth, for family offices, investors, and individuals entering a significant financial relationship."
problem_headline: "A convincing explanation of wealth is not the same as a verified one."
problem_statement: >-
  Family offices vetting an investment partner, individuals entering a
  serious relationship with someone whose wealth does not quite add
  up, and businesses accepting significant capital from a new investor
  all face the same underlying question: does the explanation given
  for this person's wealth actually hold up against independent
  sources, or is it simply the explanation that was given. Taking that
  explanation on trust, without verification, is a common way for
  fraud, undisclosed liabilities, or reputational risk to surface only
  after the relationship is already committed.
deliverable: >-
  An independently verified assessment of a named individual's claimed
  source of wealth, cross-referencing corporate, property and public
  record sources against the explanation given, with a clear finding
  on whether the picture holds together.
features:
  - "Business interests and directorships checked against claimed income sources"
  - "Property and asset holdings consistent, or inconsistent, with claimed wealth"
  - "Offshore structures and their disclosed or undisclosed purpose"
  - "Sanctions, adverse media and regulatory history"
  - "A clear, sourced conclusion on whether the claimed source of wealth is verifiable"
methodology: >-
  Corporate registry and property record analysis, sanctions and
  adverse media screening, and cross-referencing of claimed income
  sources against independently verifiable filings, with every finding
  verified by a named analyst before it is reported.
turnaround: "3 to 5 business days from confirmed scope."
faq:
  - q: "Is this only used for large corporate transactions?"
    a: "No, it is equally common in personal contexts: family offices vetting an investment partner, and individuals seeking clarity before a serious financial commitment to a relationship."
  - q: "What if the source of wealth cannot be fully verified?"
    a: "That is reported plainly as an inconclusive or unverified finding, which is itself valuable information for the decision in front of you."
  - q: "Can this run on an ongoing basis for a continuing relationship?"
    a: "Yes, through our Source of Wealth Monitoring subscription, which keeps this picture current for the life of an ongoing investment or relationship."
  - q: "Does this require the subject's cooperation?"
    a: "No. The report is built from publicly available information and lawfully obtained records, without the subject's knowledge or participation."
  - q: "Can this be used to satisfy a regulatory or AML obligation?"
    a: "Findings can inform your own compliance process, though the report itself is an intelligence assessment, not a substitute for your firm's formal AML source-of-funds procedure."
