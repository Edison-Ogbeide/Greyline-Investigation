problem_headline: "Serving a defendant abroad is a different, more procedurally demanding exercise than serving one in the UK."
problem_statement: >-
  Serving proceedings on a defendant resident in Nigeria or elsewhere
  in West Africa raises questions UK-based process servers are rarely
  equipped to answer: which method of service is procedurally
  recognised in that jurisdiction, how to document service to a
  standard the UK court will accept, and how to actually locate and
  reach a defendant who may not be at the address on record. Getting
  this wrong can mean a judgment is later successfully challenged for
  defective service.
deliverable: >-
  Service of UK court documents on a defendant resident in Nigeria or
  West Africa, carried out through named, vetted in-country partners
  and documented to a standard suitable for the UK court, with a
  UK-based lead investigator remaining accountable for the process
  throughout.
