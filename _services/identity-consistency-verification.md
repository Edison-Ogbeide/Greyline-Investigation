title: "Identity Consistency Verification"
slug: identity-consistency-verification
description: "Verification that a person's claimed identity, name, age, location, background, is consistent across records, before you commit to trusting it."
problem_headline: "A name and a photo are the easiest parts of an identity to fake."
problem_statement: >-
  Whether meeting someone online, entering a new personal relationship,
  or dealing with someone whose background does not quite add up, the
  most basic question is often the hardest to answer alone: is this
  person actually who they say they are. Names, ages, locations and
  claimed employment can all be checked against independent sources,
  but doing that thoroughly and lawfully requires a structured
  approach most people are not equipped to carry out themselves.
deliverable: >-
  A structured verification of a named individual's claimed identity,
  cross-referencing name, age, location, employment and other claimed
  details against publicly available and lawfully obtained records,
  with a clear finding on consistency.
features:
  - "Name, age and location claims tested against independent sources"
  - "Employment and professional claims verified where checkable"
  - "Cross-platform identity checks for inconsistent or duplicate profiles"
  - "Photo provenance checks where images are part of the claimed identity"
  - "A clear, documented finding on overall consistency"
methodology: >-
  Lawful open-source and public record research, cross-platform
  identity checks, and photo provenance analysis where relevant, with
  every finding sourced and reviewed by a named analyst before it is
  reported.
turnaround: "3 to 5 business days from confirmed scope."
faq:
  - q: "Can this confirm someone's real name if they have been using a false one?"
    a: "In many cases, yes, through cross-referencing publicly available records and cross-platform activity, though results depend on the specific circumstances and are reported honestly."
  - q: "How is this different from Relationship Authenticity Verification?"
    a: "This service focuses specifically on identity, whether the name, age, location and background claimed actually check out. Relationship Authenticity Verification is broader and also assesses the relationship itself for indicators of deception."
  - q: "Does this include checking photographs for authenticity?"
    a: "Where photographs are a significant part of the claimed identity, reverse image and provenance checks are included, findings worded as consistent, or not consistent, with the claimed source rather than as a definitive verdict."
  - q: "Will this work if I only have a first name and a photo?"
    a: "It is possible to start with limited information, though the strength of the finding depends on what is available. This is discussed candidly on your scoping call."
  - q: "Is this conducted without the subject's knowledge?"
    a: "Yes. The verification is built from publicly available information and lawfully obtained records, without contacting the subject."
