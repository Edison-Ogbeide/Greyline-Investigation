description: "Sustained monthly intelligence on a named subject — continuous monitoring, material-change flagging, and a monthly intelligence digest, for the situations one report can't resolve."
problem_headline: "You need to know what changes over time, not just a snapshot."
problem_statement: >-
  A point-in-time investigation answers who someone was on the day the
  report was written. Continuous Subject Monitoring answers who they
  continue to be — sustained intelligence on a named subject, with material
  changes flagged as they occur and a comprehensive digest delivered at the
  end of every monitoring period. Built for the situations where one report
  isn't enough: a spouse with sustained concerns, a family member watching
  for renewed romance-fraud targeting, a diaspora family wanting ongoing
  visibility into a relative's situation abroad.
deliverable: >-
  Every engagement is configured around a named monitoring focus, agreed at
  scoping and adjustable at any time through a direct analyst channel.
  Categories can be combined and include infidelity indicators, undisclosed
  adult-platform or gambling activity, hidden identities and aliases, broad
  behavioural drift, reputational mentions, and — where the subject is a
  minor — online predator and harmful-content exposure, handled with a
  dedicated safeguarding-first standard.
features:
  - "Dating-app and hidden-account monitoring: new registrations, reactivated accounts, and secondary social profiles not previously disclosed"
  - "Location and lifestyle anomaly signals cross-referenced against stated routine"
  - "Reputation and mention monitoring across news, forums and review platforms, triaged so clients aren't notified of irrelevant noise"
  - "For minors: grooming-pattern and sextortion-attempt monitoring, and exposure to self-harm, extremist or age-inappropriate content — every finding delivered with welfare and safeguarding as the priority, not a punitive framing"
  - "End-of-period intelligence digest: what changed, what stayed stable, and what warrants attention, every finding sourced and dated"
  - "Direct named-analyst channel for questions or scope changes between digests"
methodology: >-
  A single named analyst owns the monitoring engagement throughout. Standard
  monitoring is structured around the agreed reporting cycle; where a
  finding cannot reasonably wait for the next digest, an enhanced real-time
  alert option is available so a material finding — a new alias, a
  reactivated account, a confirmed exposure event — reaches you within
  hours, with the analyst ready to scope next steps immediately, including
  court-ready evidence packaging where the finding has legal implications.
turnaround: "First digest delivered at the close of the first monitoring period; real-time alerts, where enabled, within hours of a material finding during business hours."
faq:
  - q: "Can the monitoring focus be changed after it starts?"
    a: "Yes — scope is agreed at outset but can be adjusted at any time through your direct analyst channel, and the engagement can be ended by agreement rather than locked into a fixed term."
  - q: "How are findings involving a minor handled?"
    a: "With a dedicated safeguarding standard: all findings are delivered with sensitivity to the minor's welfare, and recommended next steps focus on safeguarding outcomes rather than a punitive response."
