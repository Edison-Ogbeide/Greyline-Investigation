description: "A consolidated evidence and intelligence pack assembled before proceedings are issued, so a legal team enters a dispute with a defensible factual base."
problem_headline: "Building a case's evidential foundation after proceedings begin is slower and more expensive than building it before."
problem_statement: >-
  Legal teams preparing to issue proceedings often need several
  strands of factual groundwork assembled quickly: a party's asset
  position, forensically preserved digital evidence, witness
  availability, and the corporate structure of an opposing party.
  Defamation matters carry an additional, more time-sensitive need:
  documenting who published a defamatory statement, how far it spread,
  and what harm resulted, before a letter before action is sent.
  Requesting these strands separately, at different points as the case
  develops, is slower and costs more in coordination than commissioning
  a single, consolidated pack scoped to the specific matter before
  proceedings are issued.
deliverable: >-
  A consolidated pre-litigation pack combining the specific intelligence
  strands your matter requires, asset visibility, forensically sound
  digital evidence preservation, corporate structure analysis, witness
  location, or defamation-specific evidence of publication and reach,
  into one coordinated engagement delivered ahead of issuing proceedings.
features:
  - "Scoped to the specific strands your matter actually needs"
  - "Forensically sound digital evidence capture with full metadata, timestamp verification and chain-of-custody documentation where a matter turns on preserving web, social media or communications content before it can be altered or deleted"
  - "Defamation-specific evidence of publication, authorship and reach where the matter is heading toward a letter before action or defamation claim"
  - "One named analyst coordinating all strands rather than separate engagements"
  - "Delivered as a single, structured pack for your legal team"
  - "Findings sourced and documented to evidential standard throughout"
  - "Scoped and timed to your pre-action timetable"

  Combines our standard methodology for each relevant strand, asset
  tracing, forensic digital evidence capture and preservation, corporate
  research, witness location, open-source identity research into
  anonymous or pseudonymous publishers where a matter is
  defamation-specific, coordinated by a single named analyst and
  delivered as one consolidated pack rather than separate disconnected
  reports.
