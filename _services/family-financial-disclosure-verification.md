title: "Family Financial Disclosure Verification"
slug: family-financial-disclosure-verification
description: "Independent verification of a spouse's financial disclosure in divorce proceedings, checking it against publicly available and lawfully obtained records."
problem_headline: "Financial disclosure in divorce proceedings is only as reliable as the person providing it."
problem_statement: >-
  Form E and equivalent financial disclosure in divorce proceedings
  relies substantially on self-reporting. A spouse motivated to
  understate assets or income has both the opportunity and, in
  contested proceedings, sometimes the incentive to do so, and standard
  disclosure review does not always catch undisclosed business
  interests, property, or income sources that do not appear in the
  paperwork provided. Independent verification closes that gap before
  a financial settlement is agreed on incomplete information.
deliverable: >-
  An independent verification of a spouse's financial disclosure,
  cross-referencing declared assets, business interests and income
  against publicly available and lawfully obtained records, and
  flagging any material inconsistency for your solicitor's attention.
features:
  - "Corporate and directorship interests not disclosed in Form E"
  - "Property ownership, current and recently transferred"
  - "Lifestyle and spending patterns inconsistent with declared income"
  - "Offshore structures or interests not fully disclosed"
  - "A clear summary of material inconsistencies for your solicitor"
methodology: >-
  Corporate registry and property record analysis, cross-referenced
  against the declared financial disclosure, combined with open-source
  research into lifestyle indicators where relevant, with every
  finding verified by a named analyst before it is reported.
turnaround: "5 to 7 business days from confirmed scope."
faq:

  - q: "Can this identify assets held offshore?"
    a: "Where offshore structures leave a traceable footprint through connected UK entities or public records, this is included as part of the scope."
  - q: "How does this connect to your Fraud & Asset Intelligence capability?"

Where the matter is not a family proceeding, tracing hidden assets in a fraud, dispute or recovery context, our Fraud & Asset Intelligence capability covers that broader work, including its own lifestyle evidence module where spending needs documenting as standalone evidence."
  - q: "Will the other spouse know this has been commissioned?"
    a: "No. It is built entirely from publicly available information and lawfully obtained records, without contacting the other party."
  - q: "Can findings be used directly in court?"
    a: "Findings are documented and sourced to a standard suitable for your solicitor to use in proceedings, though how they are deployed is a matter for your legal team's strategy."
