description: "Forensically sound capture and preservation of digital evidence, websites, social media, communications, before it can be altered or deleted."
problem_headline: "Digital evidence is easy to lose and easy to have challenged if it was not captured properly."
problem_statement: >-
  Website content, social media posts and online communications can be
  edited or deleted within minutes of a party realising they are
  relevant to a dispute. A screenshot taken casually, without
  timestamp verification, source metadata, or a documented chain of
  custody, is vulnerable to challenge on authenticity grounds precisely
  when its content matters most. Evidence that is not preserved
  properly at the point it is found may not exist by the time
  proceedings begin.
deliverable: >-
  Forensically sound capture and preservation of specified digital
  evidence, with full metadata, timestamp verification, and a
  documented chain of custody, prepared to a standard that withstands
  a challenge to authenticity.

  - "Timestamped, metadata-preserved capture of web pages and social media content"
  - "Archival of content at risk of deletion or alteration"
  - "Full chain-of-custody documentation from capture to delivery"
  - "Verification methodology disclosed to support authenticity if challenged"
  - "Delivered in a format ready for exhibiting in proceedings"
methodology: >-
  Forensic capture tools that preserve full metadata and timestamp
  integrity, combined with documented chain-of-custody procedures from
  the moment of capture, with every capture verified by a named
  analyst before delivery.
turnaround: "24 to 48 hours from confirmed scope, expedited capture available where content is at immediate risk of deletion."
