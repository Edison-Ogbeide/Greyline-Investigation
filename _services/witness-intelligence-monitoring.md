description: "Continuous monitoring of a witness's location, credibility indicators and public activity throughout the life of a dispute."
problem_headline: "A witness's circumstances can change between location and trial."
problem_statement: >-
  Witness Location & Credibility Verification establishes where a witness
  is and what is known about their credibility at a fixed point in time.
  Witnesses relocate, make public statements, appear in court in
  unrelated matters, or develop new business interests, and a legal team
  is often the last to know when any of this happens.
deliverable: >-
  Continuous monitoring of a named witness's location, public statements
  and credibility indicators, with a monthly intelligence briefing and
  priority alerts issued directly when a material development is
  confirmed.
features:
  - "Changes in witness location"
  - "New business interests or affiliations"
  - "Public statements and social media activity"
  - "Court appearances and adverse media developments"
methodology: >-
  Continuous open-source and public records monitoring focused on the
  named witness, with every candidate finding verified by a named analyst
  before it is reported.
turnaround: "First briefing at the close of the first monitoring period; priority alerts issued within hours of a confirmed material development."
faq:
  - q: "How does this differ from a one-off Witness Location & Credibility Verification instruction?"
    a: "That instruction locates a witness and verifies credibility at a point in time. This subscription keeps that picture current for as long as the witness remains relevant to the matter."
  - q: "Can this run up to and through trial?"
    a: "Yes. It is commonly instructed from the point a witness is identified through to the conclusion of proceedings."
