title: "Continuous Litigation Intelligence"
slug: continuous-litigation-intelligence
description: "A fully managed litigation intelligence service combining evidence, witness, defendant and settlement monitoring into a single subscription."
problem_headline: "Long-running disputes need more than one strand of intelligence watched at a time."
problem_statement: >-
  A complex dispute rarely develops on a single front. Evidence changes,
  witnesses relocate, defendants move assets across borders, and
  settlement leverage shifts, often at the same time and often without
  any one person on the legal team seeing the full picture. Instructing
  each strand separately, evidence monitoring from one instruction,
  witness tracking from another, defendant asset visibility from a
  third, creates gaps between them and duplicated coordination effort
  for a legal team that already has enough to manage. A long-running
  matter needs one team holding the whole picture, not several
  disconnected watchers each seeing only their own slice of it.
deliverable: >-
  A single managed subscription combining evidence monitoring, witness
  monitoring, and defendant & asset monitoring, with its modular
  international and settlement layers, with a periodic litigation
  briefing consolidating all strands and immediate alerts issued
  directly when a significant development is confirmed in any of them.
  Where your matter genuinely needs continuous coverage across more
  than one front, this replaces separate subscriptions with a single
  point of contact and one consolidated view.
features:
  - "Evidence monitoring: new or deleted digital evidence, website and social media changes, public record developments"
  - "Witness monitoring: location changes, credibility indicators, adverse media and court appearances"
  - "Defendant & asset monitoring, including the international and settlement layers where instructed"
  - "A single named analyst coordinating all instructed strands"
  - "Periodic litigation briefings consolidating all strands into one report"
  - "Immediate alerts on significant developments in any strand"
methodology: >-
  Continuous monitoring across all instructed strands, drawing on the
  same underlying sources and verification standard as each standalone
  monitoring service, coordinated by a single named analyst who
  consolidates findings into one periodic briefing rather than several
  disconnected reports arriving on different schedules from different
  points of contact.
turnaround: "First consolidated briefing at the close of the first monitoring period; priority alerts issued within hours of a confirmed significant development in any strand."
faq:
  - q: "Can we start with one strand and add others later?"
    a: "Yes. Many clients begin with evidence or defendant & asset monitoring and add witness monitoring or the settlement layer as the dispute develops and its shape becomes clearer."
  - q: "Is this priced differently to instructing each monitoring service separately?"
    a: "Scope and structure are confirmed on your scoping call, tailored to which strands your matter actually needs."
  - q: "What happens to the strand-specific detail? Do we lose visibility into, say, witness developments specifically?"
    a: "No. The consolidated briefing is structured by strand so each is still clearly visible, the value is in having them delivered together, coordinated by one analyst, rather than in losing the strand-level detail."
  - q: "Who is this best suited to?"
    a: "Legal teams and family offices managing a long-running, multi-front dispute, where evidence, a witness, a defendant's position and settlement leverage are all realistically capable of shifting before the matter concludes."
