---
title: "Counterparty Intelligence Report"
slug: counterparty-intelligence-report
description: "Analyst-led due diligence on a business counterparty, joint venture partner, or new supplier, before you sign, invest, or extend credit."
problem_headline: "Standard company searches tell you what a counterparty says about itself."
problem_statement: >-
  A Companies House printout and a credit reference confirm that a
  business exists and files its accounts. They do not tell you whether
  its beneficial owners have a history of insolvency under other
  company names, whether it is party to undisclosed litigation, whether
  its stated trading relationships are genuine, or whether the people
  behind it have interests that conflict with yours. Entering a
  contract, joint venture, or credit relationship on that basis alone
  is a decision made with a fraction of the available picture.
deliverable: >-
  A structured intelligence report on the counterparty organisation and
  its key individuals, covering corporate structure, beneficial
  ownership, litigation and insolvency history, adverse media, and
  significant undisclosed connections, delivered with sourced findings
  and a clear risk assessment.
features:
  - "Beneficial ownership traced through intermediate holding entities"
  - "Prior insolvencies, disqualifications and dissolved companies linked to key individuals"
  - "Litigation history, current and historical, involving the entity or its principals"
  - "Undisclosed related-party relationships and conflicts of interest"
methodology: >-
  Corporate registry analysis across current and historical filings,
  court record searches, adverse media screening, and open-source
  verification of claimed trading relationships, with every finding
  cross-referenced against at least one independent source and
  verified by a named analyst before it is reported.
turnaround: "3 to 5 business days from confirmed scope, expedited review available for time-sensitive transactions."
faq:
  - q: "Is this different from a standard credit check or company search?"
    a: "Yes. A credit check assesses financial standing from filed accounts. This report investigates the people and structure behind the entity, including history that would not appear on a standard company search or credit file."
  - q: "Can this be used to support a decision not to proceed?"
    a: "Findings are documented and sourced to a standard that supports whatever decision they lead to, including walking away from a deal, and can be shared with your legal team where relevant."
  - q: "Do you need the counterparty's cooperation or knowledge?"
    a: "No. The report is built entirely from publicly available information and lawfully obtained records, without contacting the subject or alerting them to the inquiry."
  - q: "What kinds of counterparties is this used for?"
    a: "Joint venture partners, new distributors, licensing partners, major customers being extended significant credit, and any counterparty a decision is about to be made on with limited existing visibility."
