---
title: "Executive Intelligence Brief"
slug: executive-intelligence-brief
description: "Background verification and reputational due diligence on a senior executive or board candidate before appointment."
problem_headline: "The higher the role, the higher the cost of a background check that missed something."
problem_statement: >-
  Senior appointments and board seats are rarely lost to a criminal
  record a standard reference check would have caught. They are lost
  to undisclosed conflicts of interest, a pattern of departures under
  circumstances not reflected on a CV, prior directorships connected to
  insolvency, or reputational history that surfaces publicly only after
  the appointment is announced, when the cost of reversing course is
  highest. A candidate who interviews well is not the same as a
  candidate whose full history has actually been checked.
deliverable: >-
  A structured background and reputational intelligence brief on a
  named executive or board candidate, covering employment and
  directorship history, undisclosed conflicts of interest, adverse
  media, and public reputational exposure, delivered ahead of a final
  appointment decision.
features:
  - "Employment and directorship history verified against independent sources"
  - "Undisclosed conflicts of interest and competing business relationships"
  - "Adverse media and reputational exposure, current and historical"
  - "Public statements and social media activity carrying reputational risk"
  - "Prior insolvencies or director disqualifications not disclosed on a CV"
methodology: >-
  Corporate registry checks, employment and directorship verification,
  adverse media screening, and open-source reputational research,
  conducted discreetly ahead of an appointment decision, with every
  finding verified by a named analyst before it is reported.
turnaround: "3 to 5 business days from confirmed scope."
faq:
  - q: "Is this conducted discreetly, before the candidate is told?"
    a: "Yes. This work is built entirely from publicly available information and lawfully obtained records, without contacting the candidate, so it can be completed ahead of an offer being made."
  - q: "How is this different from a standard executive reference check?"
    a: "A reference check relies on referees the candidate has chosen. This brief independently verifies employment and directorship history and screens for undisclosed conflicts and adverse media that references would not surface."
  - q: "Can this be used for board appointments as well as executive hires?"
    a: "Yes, it is commonly instructed for both, and is particularly relevant where reputational exposure at board level carries wider consequences for the organisation."
  - q: "How does this differ from Candidate Risk Intelligence?"
    a: "Candidate Risk Intelligence is built for standard pre-hire vetting. This brief goes further, with a stronger focus on reputational exposure and board-level conflicts of interest appropriate to senior and public-facing appointments."
