title: "Workplace Intelligence Programme"
slug: workplace-intelligence-programme
description: "An ongoing programme of workplace risk monitoring for organisations with sustained exposure, rather than a single instructed investigation into one incident."
problem_headline: "Some organisations need more than an investigation triggered after something has already gone wrong."
problem_statement: >-
  A Workplace Investigation responds to a specific concern once it has been
  raised. Larger organisations, or those in sectors with sustained conduct
  or safeguarding exposure, often want a standing programme, a named
  analyst relationship and a documented process, rather than reassembling
  one from scratch each time a concern arises.
deliverable: >-
  An ongoing programme combining a named analyst point of contact, agreed
  triage protocols for new concerns as they are raised, and periodic
  intelligence-led review of workplace risk indicators within the agreed
  scope.
features:
  - "A named analyst as your standing point of contact for workplace concerns"
  - "Agreed triage protocols so a new concern is assessed and scoped quickly"
  - "Periodic review of documented conduct, disciplinary and safeguarding indicators"
  - "Direct escalation path into a full Workplace Investigation where warranted"
methodology: >-
  A structured, ongoing relationship rather than a single engagement,
  governed by the same evidential standard as our one-off investigations,
  with escalation to a full investigation handled to that standard when
  triggered.
turnaround: "Programme structure and triage protocols agreed at the scoping call; ongoing thereafter for the life of the programme."
faq:
  - q: "How is this different from simply instructing investigations as they come up?"
    a: "The named analyst relationship and agreed triage protocols mean a new concern is assessed against a standard already in place, rather than starting cold each time, which is materially faster when timing matters."
  - q: "Does this replace the need for our own HR investigation process?"
    a: "No. It sits alongside your internal process, providing intelligence-led investigative capability and escalation where your own process identifies the need for it."
