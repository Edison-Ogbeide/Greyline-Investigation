slug: executive-exposure-monitoring
description: "Continuous monitoring of executive digital footprints, credential exposure, impersonation attempts and reputational risk for leadership teams under sustained risk, with a periodic board-level review built in."
problem_headline: "Executives remain a target long after a single exposure review ends."
problem_statement: >-
  An Executive Exposure Profile documents an executive's digital footprint
  and exposure as it stood at the time of review. Executives who are
  publicly visible, involved in high-value transactions, or otherwise
  higher profile remain targets on an ongoing basis, and new exposure, a
  credential leak, an impersonation attempt, a doxxing incident, an
  undisclosed conflict of interest, can emerge at any point afterward.
  Boards that review financial risk quarterly rarely review the executive
  team's personal exposure with the same discipline, and that gap tends to
  surface only once something has already gone wrong.
deliverable: >-
  Continuous monitoring of a named executive's digital footprint,
  credential exposure and impersonation risk, with a monthly intelligence
  briefing and priority alerts issued directly when a material development
  is confirmed, plus a periodic board-ready review, agreed at quarterly
  or biannual cycle, summarising adverse media, reputational exposure and
  undisclosed conflicts of interest for the leadership team as a whole.
features:
  - "Changes to the executive's public digital footprint"
  - "Credential exposure connected to the executive"
  - "Impersonation attempts and doxxing activity"
  - "High-risk social media exposure and personal information leaks"
  - "Adverse media and reputational exposure reviewed on an agreed cycle"
  - "Undisclosed conflicts of interest or business relationships, where publicly discoverable"
methodology: >-
  Continuous open-source and dark web monitoring focused on the named
  executive, with every candidate finding verified by a named analyst
  before it is reported. The periodic board review draws on the same
  monitoring data, consolidated into a prioritised, board-ready summary
  rather than an undifferentiated list of everything found.
turnaround: "First monthly briefing at the close of the first monitoring period; priority alerts issued within hours of a confirmed material development. The periodic board review is delivered on the agreed cycle, typically quarterly or biannually."
faq:

  - q: "Who typically instructs this?"
    a: "Boards and security teams protecting named executives who are publicly visible, involved in high-value transactions, or have previously been targeted."
  - q: "Can this cover the whole leadership team, not just one executive?"
    a: "Yes. Scope is agreed at the outset based on your leadership team's size and the board's priorities, and the periodic review is designed to summarise exposure across the whole team, not just a single individual."
  - q: "Is the periodic board review the same as a live board briefing?"
    a: "The review itself is a written, board-ready summary. Where a live presentation to the board is also wanted, that is delivered through our Intelligence Briefings service under Corporate Intelligence."
