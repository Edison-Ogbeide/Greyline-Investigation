title: "Asset Recovery Intelligence (Post-Judgment)"
slug: asset-recovery-intelligence
description: "Locating and documenting a judgment debtor's assets across the UK and internationally, to support enforcement action after a judgment has been won."
problem_headline: "A judgment is a piece of paper until you can point an enforcement agent at something real."
problem_statement: >-
  Winning a judgment establishes that a debt is owed. It does not
  locate the money. Judgment debtors who intend to avoid paying
  routinely move assets into other names, restructure ownership, or
  transfer value offshore, often before or during proceedings, in
  anticipation of losing. Enforcement agents, charging orders and
  freezing applications all depend on knowing what to point at, and
  that intelligence rarely arrives on its own.
deliverable: >-
  A detailed asset tracing report identifying the judgment debtor's
  known assets, UK and international, including property, company
  interests, bank relationships where lawfully identifiable, and
  patterns of transfer to connected parties, structured to support
  enforcement action directly.
features:
  - "Property and company interests, current and recently transferred"
  - "Connected parties who may be holding assets on the debtor's behalf"
  - "International assets, where cross-border reach applies"
  - "Patterns of transfer consistent with deliberate dissipation"
  - "Findings structured to support charging orders, third-party debt orders, or enforcement agents directly"
methodology: >-
  Property and corporate registry analysis, court record searches for
  related proceedings, and network analysis of connected parties and
  transfer patterns, drawing on our cross-border capability where
  assets have moved outside the UK, with every finding verified by a
  named analyst before it is reported.
turnaround: "5 to 10 business days from confirmed scope, depending on the complexity of the debtor's structure."
faq:
  - q: "What happens if the debtor has moved assets to family members?"
    a: "This is common and the report is built to surface it. Assets held by connected parties on a debtor's behalf can still be relevant to enforcement, and findings are documented accordingly."
  - q: "Can this support an application to set aside a transaction?"
    a: "Findings are documented to an evidential standard that can support an application to challenge a transaction as a transfer at undervalue, in consultation with your legal team."
  - q: "Does this cover assets outside the UK?"
    a: "Yes, where there is reason to believe assets have moved internationally, with particular reach into the Nigeria and West Africa corridor through named in-country networks."
  - q: "Is there a minimum judgment value for this to be worthwhile?"
    a: "There is no fixed threshold. The right question is whether the likely recoverable value justifies the cost of tracing, which we discuss candidly on your scoping call before you commit."
