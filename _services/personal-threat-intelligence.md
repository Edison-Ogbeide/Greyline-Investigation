title: "Personal Threat Intelligence"
slug: personal-threat-intelligence
description: "An intelligence assessment of a specific online threat, harassment, or stalking concern, to understand who is behind it and what risk it carries."
problem_headline: "Online harassment feels personal and immediate. Understanding who is behind it rarely is, without help."
problem_statement: >-
  Being targeted by online harassment, stalking, or coordinated abuse
  is frightening precisely because the person behind it is often
  unclear, hidden behind anonymous accounts, and their intent and
  actual risk level are hard to judge from inside the situation. Police
  reports require specifics that victims often cannot provide alone.
  An intelligence assessment brings a structured, evidence-based
  approach to identifying who is involved, whether the threat is
  escalating, and what steps are proportionate.
deliverable: >-
  A structured threat assessment of a specific harassment, stalking, or
  online abuse concern, identifying the individuals involved where
  possible, assessing escalation risk, and providing clear,
  proportionate recommendations for next steps.
features:
  - "Identification of individuals behind anonymous accounts, where achievable"
  - "Assessment of escalation risk based on documented behaviour patterns"
  - "Evidence gathered and preserved to support a police report if needed"
  - "Coordinated network or pattern of abuse identified where present"
  - "Clear, proportionate recommendations, not alarmist or dismissive"
methodology: >-
  Lawful open-source and dark web research into the individuals and
  patterns behind the reported activity, assessed against recognised
  threat escalation indicators, with every finding verified by a named
  analyst before it is reported.
turnaround: "48 to 72 hours from confirmed scope, given the often time-sensitive nature of personal threat concerns."
faq:
  - q: "Can this identify who is behind an anonymous account?"

  - q: "Should I go to the police first or instruct this first?"
    a: "Either can come first depending on your situation. Many clients find a structured assessment strengthens a police report; others instruct us after an initial report did not progress. We can advise on the right order for your circumstances."

    a: "You are contacted directly and immediately if a finding suggests urgent risk, with clear guidance on next steps including engaging law enforcement, not held back for a scheduled report."
  - q: "Can this continue if the harassment does not stop?"
    a: "Yes, through our Personal Threat Monitoring subscription, which continues tracking the situation for as long as the concern remains live."
  - q: "Is this only for individuals, or can businesses use this too?"
    a: "This specific service is scoped for personal threats against individuals. Organisational threats are covered separately under our Cyber & Digital Risk Intelligence capability."
