problem_statement: >-
  Relationship Authenticity Verification establishes a picture at a
  point in time. Relationships that continue over months or years can
  see new online profiles, identity inconsistencies, or adverse media
  emerge afterward, often without the client being told.
deliverable: >-
  Continuous monitoring of public records, social media activity and
  identity consistency connected to a named individual, with a monthly
  intelligence briefing and priority alerts issued directly when a
  material development is confirmed.
features:
  - "New public records and identity changes"
  - "New online profiles and social media activity"
  - "Ongoing identity consistency checks against the details you were given"
  - "Adverse media developments"
  - "Significant digital developments affecting the relationship"
methodology: >-
  Continuous open-source and public records monitoring, cross-referenced
  against the baseline picture established at verification, with every
  candidate finding verified by a named analyst before it is reported.
turnaround: "First briefing at the close of the first monitoring period; priority alerts issued within hours of a confirmed material development."
faq:
  - q: "Does this require a completed verification first?"
    a: "It is the usual starting point, since the initial verification establishes the baseline picture that this subscription then monitors for change."
  - q: "Is this conducted discreetly?"
    a: "Yes. All monitoring uses publicly available information and lawfully obtained records, and reporting is handled with the same discretion as our one-off verification work."
  - q: "What kind of developments actually trigger a priority alert, rather than waiting for the monthly briefing?"
    a: "A new online profile inconsistent with what you were told, an identity detail that no longer checks out, or adverse media directly relevant to the relationship. Lower-significance developments are still captured, but held for the periodic briefing rather than sent as an interruption."
  - q: "Can this be paused or ended if the relationship situation changes?"
    a: "Yes. This is a subscription, not a fixed-term commitment, and can be adjusted or ended by agreement as your circumstances change."
  - q: "Can this be combined with monitoring on the other party's business or financial interests?"
    a: "Where a relationship carries a business or financial dimension, our Fraud & Asset Intelligence capability's Asset & Wealth Monitoring can run alongside this subscription to cover that separately."
  - q: "How is this different from Digital Identity & Threat Monitoring?"
    a: "This service is scoped specifically to a relationship and the individual in it, identity, profiles and adverse media connected to that person. Digital Identity & Threat Monitoring is broader and covers your own identity, data exposure and personal threat, and the two can run alongside each other where both are relevant."
