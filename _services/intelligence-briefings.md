  Ahead of a material decision, an acquisition, a market entry, a response

  of the people making the decision. Where the audience is a board rather

  around governance and the decision at hand, concise and sourced, not the
  full investigative detail.

  A confidential intelligence briefing prepared for boards, executive

  and a presentation with the instructing analyst, followed by questions
  and answers. Available in a board-ready format: the same findings,
  sourcing and confidence levels, reframed explicitly around the
  governance decision in question and prepared for inclusion in board
  papers, whether as a standalone briefing or as a reporting layer added
  to an underlying engagement you have already commissioned.

  - "Board-ready format available: governance framing, sourcing and confidence levels preserved, suitable for board papers"

  Prepared to the same evidential and sourcing standard as our written

  adapted for a board audience rather than the underlying findings being
  altered.
