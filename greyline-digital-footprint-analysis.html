The question this answers

"What is this person's complete digital presence, and does it match what I've been told?"

The Greyline Digital Footprint Analysis is a comprehensive, analyst-led sweep of a named subject's entire online presence — and, importantly, an analytical breakdown of what that presence reveals. Most online research products stop at returning a list of URLs. The Digital Footprint Analysis goes further: we map the footprint, score it against risk indicators, test consistency against the story you've been told, and produce a deliverable a non-technical reader can act on.

The product is offered in three tiers. Tier 1 (£189) covers the standard sweep — social media, dating platforms, public mentions, posted images, and a structured findings report. Tier 2 (£449) adds digital behaviour analysis — posting patterns, network of interactions, language and timezone signatures, platform-by-platform consistency. Tier 3 (£899) adds the Digital Behaviour Intelligence Report — a deeper analytical review of the subject's digital persona, including assessments of authenticity, consistency, and risk pattern matching against the relationship or commercial context.

What is included

The full scope of what we examine.

  • Full social media audit. Every major platform — Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), TikTok, Snapchat, YouTube, Pinterest. Public posts, photo galleries, friends and connections, group memberships, location check-ins, and historical activity.
  • Username and alias discovery. The subject's known handles are searched across 300+ platforms — including niche forums, professional networks, dating sites, gaming platforms, and platforms specific to the diaspora communities relevant to the subject.
  • Dating platform sweep. Tinder, Bumble, Hinge, Badoo, Plenty of Fish, Match, eHarmony, OkCupid, Coffee Meets Bagel, and the diaspora-specific platforms that mainstream tools ignore (Afroromance, Naijaplanet, and others).
  • Reverse image search. Every public-facing photograph is reverse-searched. We are looking for unauthorised use of someone else's photographs, recurring use across multiple dating profiles, and visual signatures of AI-generated or stolen images.
  • Forum, directory, and public mention checks. Beyond the obvious platforms, we sweep professional directories, court records where lawful, complaints databases, news mentions, and forum posts referencing the subject.
  • Risk-scored analytical report. The deliverable is the analysis, not a screenshot dump. Findings are scored across consistency, authenticity, risk pattern matching, and red flag indicators. The report explains what was found, why it matters, and what it implies for the question you brought to us.
  • Tier 2 and Tier 3 add-ons. Behavioural analysis (posting cadence, network mapping, language analysis, timezone signatures) at Tier 2; full Digital Behaviour Intelligence Report (deep persona analysis, authenticity assessment, structured risk profile) at Tier 3.
