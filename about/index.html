WHAT WE WILL NOT DO

The work we refuse
defines the work we accept.

Most firms have a list of things they will not do. Few publish it. Ours is published because the boundary is the protection, for the client, the subject, and the firm.

  • We do not hack accounts or intercept private communications. All work is conducted on publicly available information, lawfully obtained records, or material disclosed with informed consent.
  • We do not impersonate individuals to extract information. Pretexting and false identity outreach are not part of our methodology.
  • We do not pay for unlawfully obtained data. Stolen data and breach dumps are excluded categorically, even where a client would pay separately for them.
  • We do not accept enforcement side immigration instruction. Our pre visa relationship integrity work is commissioned by the sponsor only.
  • We do not inflate findings to justify a fee. A clean finding is reported as a clean finding.
  • We do not take work that requires misleading a court, regulator or tribunal. Reports are prepared to the standard the proceedings require.
  • We do not discuss live or historical cases publicly. We do not confirm or deny client relationships to third parties.
