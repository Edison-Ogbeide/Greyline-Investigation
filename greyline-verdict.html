Who Instructs This Service

Real situations, real clients.

Greyline Verdict is built for moments where the client needs an answer before doing something they can't easily reverse — sending money, booking flights, or formally introducing the person into their wider life.

Scenario One
The online match before the first meeting

You've connected on a dating app or social platform. Conversation has progressed. Before you meet in person — or invest more emotionally — you want to know they're real and safe.

Scenario Two
The long-distance partner asking for help

A long-distance partner, often abroad, has begun asking for money, gifts, or financial involvement. The story might be true. Before sending anything, you want certainty.

Scenario Three
The travel commitment

You're being asked to fly out, host the person, or sponsor a visit. The cost — financial and personal — is significant enough to verify before committing.

Scenario Four
The much-younger or much-older partner

An age, financial, or circumstantial mismatch is creating concern — yours, or your family's. A formal verification can either lay concerns to rest or reveal what should not proceed.

Scenario Five
The diaspora courtship

You're courted by — or courting — someone in your home country (or vice versa) and need verification before introducing the person to family or making commitments across borders.

Scenario Six
The instinct that something's off

You can't articulate why, but something doesn't add up. Photos, stories, timing, behaviour. You don't need to justify the instinct — Greyline Verdict will find what's behind it, or confirm there's nothing to find.

Frequently Asked Questions

Things people need to know before instructing.

Is this lawful? Do I need the subject's consent?

Greyline Verdict relies on open-source intelligence and lawfully accessible data sources. We do not require the subject's consent because we do not access private accounts, intercept communications, or use any technique that would require it. We operate under the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and GDPR (UK) on a confirmed lawful basis — the legitimate interests of a client verifying someone they are personally engaging with.

Where any element of the engagement would require the subject's knowledge or consent, we say so before accepting instruction. We do not undertake work that crosses lawful lines.

Will the subject know they have been investigated?

No. The work is conducted entirely from open and lawful sources, leaves no detectable trace with the subject, and is delivered to you confidentially. The subject is not contacted, notified, or alerted at any stage.

What if the subject is in another country?

Greyline Verdict is built for international subjects. Most romance scams and online deception cases involve cross-border parties. Our methodology and source coverage are not UK-only — we routinely verify subjects in West Africa, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Eastern Europe, and elsewhere.

For cases requiring physical, in-country confirmation (visiting a stated address, confirming an employer in person), that work is outside the scope of Greyline Verdict and would be handled under our Field Relationship Investigation engagement.

What information do I need to provide?

The minimum we need is a name and at least one of: a phone number, email address, dating-app handle, or social media link. The more identifiers you can share — photos they've sent, the platform you met on, the country they say they're in, anything notable about the conversation — the more precise the verification.

Once you instruct, we send a secure intake form covering everything we need. You're not expected to know what's relevant — we'll ask the right questions.

What happens if the verdict is Red?

The report sets out the verdict and the evidence behind it clearly. You receive a recommended next-steps section — which may include disengaging immediately, preserving evidence in case of future reporting, contacting the platform you met on, or in serious cases, reporting to Action Fraud or the relevant authority.

Where you want further investigation — for example, identifying the actual person behind the account, or tracing money already sent — that becomes a Fraud Investigation engagement, scoped separately.

What if the verdict is Amber?

Amber means the evidence is mixed — there are signals worth taking seriously, but no single finding is definitive. The report will spell out exactly what concerns us and what we found reassuring. You retain full discretion to decide how to proceed; we provide the intelligence, you make the decision.

In most Amber cases, clients choose to either pause and ask the subject specific questions informed by our findings, or escalate to a deeper Footprint Analysis (Tier 2 or Tier 3) for fuller picture before committing further.

Can the report be used in legal proceedings?

The Greyline Verdict report is built primarily to support your personal decision, not as evidential material for court. For matters where the intelligence will be used in proceedings, we recommend engaging us under our Litigation Support service — where the chain-of-custody, methodology documentation, and evidential standards required by court are built into the engagement from the outset.

How do you handle my data?

Engagement data is stored encrypted, accessed only by the assigned analyst, and retained for the period set out in our Data Protection & Confidentiality Policy. The report is delivered via encrypted secure link. After delivery, working files are securely destroyed unless retention is required to support an active onward engagement.
