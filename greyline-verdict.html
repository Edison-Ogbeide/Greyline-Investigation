The Question This Answers

"They seem real. But how do I know — before I send the money, before I book the flight, before I let them deeper into my life?"

The romance scam industry now generates over a billion pounds in losses annually in the UK alone. The methodology is consistent: a stranger appears online, becomes emotionally close quickly, and at some point asks for money, travel commitment, or trust that should be earned over years.

Most victims describe the same thing afterwards — the doubts were there from the beginning, but there was nothing to anchor them to. Greyline Verdict gives you that anchor, before the irreversible decision is made.

Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers to the questions clients ask.

Will the subject know they've been investigated?

No. Greyline Verdict is conducted entirely through open-source intelligence and authorised data sources. The subject receives no notification, no contact, and no indication that any check has been carried out. Our methodology is designed to leave no trace from the subject's perspective.

Is this lawful?

Yes. Greyline operates strictly within the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and the GDPR. The Verdict uses only lawful sources of information — open-source intelligence, public records, breach-data within the limits of lawful access, and information you provide as the instructing client. We do not access private accounts, intercept communications, or use any unlawful means.

Where a check would require lawful basis you do not have (for example, formal background checks against a non-consenting third party), we do not perform it within the Verdict scope.

What does each verdict colour actually mean?

Green — the subject is verified as real, identity confirmed, no scam-pattern matches, no significant red flags. You can proceed with normal caution.

Amber — the subject appears real but specific concerns or inconsistencies have been identified. The report explains what they are and recommends specific next steps.

Red — material evidence of deception, scam-network involvement, identity fabrication, or other serious findings. The report explains what was found and strongly advises against proceeding without further investigation.

What if you find something I don't want to hear?

The Verdict reports findings as they are, regardless of whether they are welcome. We understand that engaging Greyline is an emotional decision, and we report what we find with appropriate care — but the value of the engagement is precisely that you receive the truth, even when it's uncomfortable.

If the verdict is Amber or Red, we include guidance on how to proceed. Many clients use the report as the basis for a difficult conversation rather than an immediate end to contact; the choice is always yours.

What if the result is inconclusive?

If the available intelligence does not support a confident verdict — for example, where the subject's digital footprint is unusually small, or where contradictory signals require deeper investigation — we issue an honest "inconclusive" finding and recommend the next appropriate step. This may be Footprint Analysis (Tier 2 or 3) or, where the situation warrants it, a Field Relationship Investigation.

Can you investigate someone in another country?

Yes. The Verdict is conducted on subjects regardless of location. International subjects sometimes require slightly longer turnaround due to the breadth of jurisdictional sources reviewed, and certain countries with limited public records may produce more "inconclusive" findings — in which case we are transparent about what was achievable.

How is my information handled?

All information you provide is encrypted in transit and at rest, accessible only to the analyst assigned to your case, and retained in line with our published Data Protection & Confidentiality Policy. We never share information about the engagement with anyone other than you, and we never disclose to the subject that an engagement has taken place.

What if I need a deeper investigation after the Verdict?

Many clients use the Verdict as a first step. If the findings indicate the situation warrants deeper work, we recommend the appropriate next product — typically Footprint Analysis (for digital depth), Pre-Commitment Vetting Report (for legal-grade vetting before marriage or sponsorship), or a Field Relationship Investigation (where physical confirmation is required).
