A WORD BEFORE WE BEGIN

Most clients
arrive uncertain.

A pattern that doesn't fit. A relationship that doesn't quite add up. A property purchase that suddenly feels too smooth. A message you wish you hadn't read. A name that keeps appearing where it shouldn't.

Most of our private clients come to us at exactly that moment — not certain something is wrong, but unwilling to keep wondering. Our role is to bring clarity through evidence, not assumption.

We don't take cases we can't defensibly close, and we tell you that within the first call. If we can help, we'll explain how, what it costs, and what you'll receive at the end. If we can't, we'll say so — and where possible, point you toward someone who can.

DISCRETION & SAFETY

How we
handle contact.

All initial contact is by email — through cases@greylineinvestigations.co.uk or our confidential inquiry form. We do not place unscheduled phone calls. If a call is needed, we will arrange it in advance, on a number you choose.

If you share a device with the person you are inquiring about, let us know in your first email. We will adjust our communication accordingly — including using alternative email addresses or scheduling all future contact through a different channel.

Anything you tell us in inquiry — whether or not you proceed — is held in confidence. We do not retain inquiry data beyond what is necessary, and we are registered with the ICO under UK GDPR.
