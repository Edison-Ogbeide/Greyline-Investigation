OSINT & Digital Intelligence

The digital trace work that underpins everything.

Open-source intelligence is the foundation of every Greyline engagement — the discipline that turns a name, a handle, a wallet address, or a leaked document into a structured intelligence picture. This service line is where that work is exposed directly to clients: continuous dark web monitoring for organisations carrying compliance-driven exposure risk, point-in-time dark web sweeps for individuals and businesses, and structured adverse media and background screening for the specific named subjects that compliance and integrity decisions hinge on.

From £199 · Online Checkout

Dark Web Sweep

A point-in-time investigation of dark web, paste site, and breach data sources for exposure relating to a named individual or organisation — credentials, leaked personal data, document leaks, and threat-actor mentions. Personal and business tiers available. Delivered as a structured findings report with confirmed exposures, severity ratings, and remediation guidance.

Personal £199 · Business £599
  • Dark web, paste site, and breach corpus sweep
  • Credential exposure check across known leak databases
  • Personal data exposure check (email, phone, address, full name)
  • Document and PII leak detection
  • Threat-actor mention monitoring
  • Structured findings report with remediation guidance
Suited for: individuals concerned about identity theft exposure, executives and high-net-worth individuals reviewing their dark web footprint, businesses checking exposure ahead of cyber-insurance renewal, and any client wanting a defensible point-in-time view.
