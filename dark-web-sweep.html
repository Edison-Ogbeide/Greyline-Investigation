The question this answers

"What of mine has already leaked, and where is it?"

Most clients learn that their personal or organisational data is on the dark web only when something downstream goes wrong — a credential is reused in an account takeover, a customer's data appears in a forum post, a journalist references a leak nobody internally was tracking. The Dark Web Sweep is a structured, point-in-time investigation that answers the question before something goes wrong: a defensible, documented view of what exists about you (or your organisation) across dark web markets, paste sites, breach corpora, and threat-actor channels.

The product is offered in two tiers. Personal (£199) covers a named individual — credential exposure, personal data exposure across email, phone, name and address, and threat-actor mention monitoring. Business (£599) covers a named organisation — corporate credentials, brand mentions, executive accounts, intellectual property exposure, document leaks, and structured threat-actor chatter referencing the company.

What is included

What we actually search.

  • Dark web markets and forums. Tor-based marketplaces and forum networks where credential dumps, personal data, and stolen documents are traded. We use trusted operational tradecraft and analyst-led search rather than automated scrapers that miss context.
  • Paste sites and clearnet leak boards. Pastebin and equivalents, GitHub gist abuse, and the clearnet sites where leaked data is increasingly published before reaching dark web circulation.
  • Breach corpora. The structured breach databases that aggregate credentials from confirmed data breaches — checking the subject against every major breach in scope, with timestamps and source attribution.
  • Threat-actor channels. Telegram, Discord, and the messaging-channel ecosystem where threat actors increasingly trade and discuss leaked material. Mentions of the subject (Personal tier) or the organisation, brand, or named executive (Business tier) are documented.
  • Document and PII leak detection. Beyond credentials, we look for leaked documents, leaked PII files, and leaked internal materials. Where a document or data set is found, we document its provenance and the context of its exposure.
  • Structured findings report. Confirmed exposures with severity ratings, source attribution, and remediation guidance. Where the exposure requires immediate action, we say so. Where the exposure is historic and superseded, we say that too.
