How This Service Works

What's visible online determines the risk.

Most exposure that ends up on the dark web — credentials, identity data, breached account information, leaked documents — never makes the news. It surfaces quietly in breach databases, paste sites, dark web markets, and underground forums. The Dark Web Sweep is a one-time scan that tells you, plainly, what's out there about you or your organisation. No subscription, no commitment — a single engagement to surface the truth and deliver remediation guidance.

What You Receive

A clear picture of your exposure.

The Dark Web Sweep is delivered as a structured findings report with severity ratings, contextual analyst commentary, and remediation guidance. It is built to be acted on the same day you receive it.

The Dark Web Sweep Findings Report

Personal sweeps typically run 8–12 pages; Business sweeps run 15–25 pages depending on the volume of findings. Both are delivered as encrypted PDFs via secure link, with each finding categorised by severity (Critical, High, Medium, Low) and accompanied by analyst commentary on what the finding actually means.

Where findings are clean, the report confirms that plainly. Where exposure is significant, the report sets out what's been found, where, and what action you should take to mitigate further risk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Things people need to know before instructing.

What is the dark web, and what does a sweep actually cover?

The dark web is the part of the internet not indexed by mainstream search engines and accessible only through specific software. It hosts marketplaces, forums, and paste sites where leaked data, stolen credentials, and illicit goods are traded. The Dark Web Sweep covers these sources alongside breach databases (compilations of credentials from past data breaches), paste sites (where leaked content is published), and underground forums.

What we don't do: we do not break into private systems, decrypt encrypted material, or use any technique that crosses into unauthorised access. The sweep covers what is already lawfully accessible on these sources — which is, unfortunately, often quite extensive.

Is this lawful?

Yes. Greyline operates under the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and GDPR (UK) on confirmed lawful basis — typically the legitimate interests of the client (or organisation) auditing their own exposure. Personal sweeps are conducted on identifiers belonging to the instructing client. Business sweeps are conducted under the authority of the instructing organisation. We do not sweep against third-party identifiers without lawful basis.

How is this different from free tools like HaveIBeenPwned?

Free tools tell you whether an email address has appeared in known public breach databases — useful, but a small fraction of the picture. The Greyline Dark Web Sweep covers active dark web markets, paste sites, underground forums, and breach data not indexed by free services. Findings are contextualised by an analyst and accompanied by remediation guidance. Free tools are a starting point; this is the full picture.

What happens if you find something serious?

The report flags Critical and High severity findings prominently with specific remediation guidance — credentials to change immediately, accounts to monitor for unauthorised access, identity data to alert relevant institutions about. Where appropriate, we recommend further engagements (continuous monitoring, fraud investigation, or incident response support).

Should I subscribe to ongoing monitoring instead?

The Sweep is a one-time engagement. Ongoing monitoring (Dark Web Monitoring B2B for organisations, or the relevant concern profiles within Greyline Continuous Intelligence for individuals) is appropriate where the exposure is dynamic — credentials being rotated, brand mentions changing, executive turnover, or sustained threat profile.

For most clients, the Sweep is the right entry point. It establishes the baseline; the decision to subscribe to ongoing monitoring can be made once you understand what's actually out there.

Can I commission a sweep on someone else?

Sweeps must be commissioned on identifiers the client has lawful basis to investigate. For Personal Sweeps, this is typically the client's own identifiers. For Business Sweeps, this is the organisation's domain, brand, and executives (where the organisation has authorised the work).

Sweeping a third party without their authorisation may engage data protection law and is something we decline. If you need to investigate a third party — for example, in a fraud or pre-engagement context — we have other engagements (Adverse Media & Background Checks, Pre-Hire Intelligence Report, OSINT Investigations) built for that purpose, with appropriate lawful basis structured into them.

How fast can I get the report?

Standard turnaround is 3–5 working days from instruction. Where urgency is required (active incident response, for example), expedited delivery is available — typically within 24–48 hours, quoted at the point of instruction.

What information do I need to provide?

For a Personal Sweep: name, primary email address, phone number, and any historical email addresses. For a Business Sweep: domain, organisation name, trading names, and the top five named executives you want covered. Once you instruct, we send a secure intake form covering everything we need.
