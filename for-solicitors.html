A NOTE TO INSTRUCTING ADVISORS

Built around
your case file.

Solicitors and family offices instruct us when the matter calls for intelligence that doesn't appear in standard databases — asset histories that cross jurisdictions, digital evidence that needs forensic handling, witnesses who have moved on, or counterparties whose declared position doesn't match the documentary trail.

Our investigators work to ACPO Principles for Digital Evidence and to UK GDPR. Reports are written for downstream legal use — clear chain of custody, disclosed methodology, and measured language that survives challenge.

We accept instruction on a per-matter basis or on framework terms for firms with regular needs. References available on request.

INSTRUCTION TERMS

How we
accept instruction.

Per-Matter Instruction

Standard instruction model. Letter of engagement, fixed-fee or capped-fee scoping, and a final deliverable formatted for inclusion in the case file. Most matters scoped within 24 hours of instruction.

Framework Agreements

For firms with regular volume — witness tracing, asset checks, or screening — we offer framework terms with pre-agreed rates, faster turnaround, and a single point of contact. Particularly suited to family law, insolvency, and immigration teams.

Conflict Checks

We run a conflict check before accepting any instruction. If the proposed subject is, or has been, a Greyline client, we will decline instruction and notify the instructing firm without disclosing detail.
