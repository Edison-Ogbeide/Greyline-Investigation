The Question This Answers

"They look great on paper. The references checked out. The DBS came back clean. So why does something feel off — and what am I missing?"

Standard background checks tell you what a candidate has not been formally convicted of. They tell you that the references they nominated will say complimentary things. They do not tell you who the person actually is.

Most of the meaningful risk in a senior or trust-critical hire — fabricated employment history, undisclosed business interests, social media behaviour incompatible with the role, hidden disciplinary records, conduct on prior teams that quietly led to departure — sits entirely outside what compliance-tier screening surfaces. Pre-Hire Intelligence is built to close that gap.

How It Works

From instruction to deployable report.

Step One

Instruct

Choose your tier (Standard or Executive) and complete secure online checkout. You receive immediate confirmation and access to the candidate intake portal.

Step Two

Candidate Onboarding

The candidate completes a structured consent form and information submission via secure portal. We handle the candidate-facing communication; you receive only the verified output.

Step Three

Investigation

An analyst conducts the full set of checks for the chosen tier, in parallel where possible. Standard reports typically deliver in 7–10 business days; Executive in 10–14 days due to deeper components.

Step Four

Report Delivery

An encrypted PDF report is delivered to your nominated email address. The lead analyst is available for a 15-minute follow-up call on Executive engagements to discuss findings before you act.

Who This Is For

Roles where the cost of a bad hire vastly outweighs the vetting.

Pre-Hire Intelligence is not for general workforce screening — that market is well-served by mid-tier compliance providers. It is built for roles where the integrity, judgement, or access of the hire materially affects organisational risk.

  • Standard tier: Mid-level professional hires, client-facing roles, mid-market employment with material trust expectations, or any role where the full Tier 2 footprint review adds meaningful insight beyond compliance-tier screening.
  • Executive tier: C-suite hires, partners and directors, finance and compliance leadership, household staff with family access (PAs, nannies, drivers, estate managers), regulated-industry hires.
  • Executive tier: Foreign nationals where source-country verification is essential, foundational team hires at startups (early employees with deep system and capital access), and any candidate where adverse media or PEP/sanctions screening is operationally required.
  • Executive tier: Schools, care providers, and any organisation hiring staff with access to vulnerable people — where surface-level DBS does not constitute adequate due diligence.
Frequently Asked Questions

The questions HR, legal, and hiring managers ask before instructing.

Is candidate consent required?

Yes, for the formal verification components (criminal record checks, employment verification, education verification, reference checks). Candidates complete a structured consent form during onboarding that authorises the specific checks within the tier they're being vetted for.

The digital footprint and OSINT components use only lawful open-source and public sources, but candidate consent to the overall engagement is still obtained as a matter of good practice and GDPR transparency.

How does this comply with GDPR?

The engagement is structured under GDPR Article 6(1)(b) (necessary for the performance of a contract — i.e., the prospective employment relationship) and Article 6(1)(f) (legitimate interests of the prospective employer). Candidate consent is documented in writing for the formal verification components. Candidates have full data subject rights, and Greyline operates under our published Data Protection & Confidentiality Policy throughout.

Which tier should I choose?

Standard is appropriate for the majority of trust-critical professional hires — mid-level finance, operations, compliance, client-facing roles, and any position where the Tier 2 footprint review materially adds to standard screening. Most clients commissioning their first Greyline hire vetting use Standard.

Executive is appropriate when the role's risk profile genuinely warrants the full investigative depth — C-suite, partners, household staff with family access, foreign nationals, regulated-industry hires, and any position where civil litigation, directorships, or PEP/sanctions screening is operationally relevant.

How does this compare to standard background-check providers?

Standard providers (Sterling, HireRight, Verifile, etc.) deliver compliance-tier screening — DBS check, employment verification, education verification, basic right-to-work — typically within £100–£200 per candidate. They do not include digital footprint review, behavioural intelligence, hidden account discovery, or analyst-led interpretation.

Pre-Hire Intelligence Standard at £499 includes everything those providers deliver plus a Tier 2 digital footprint review — making it more thorough than the compliance-tier market while remaining productised. Pre-Hire Intelligence Executive sits between mid-market vetting and boutique investigative firms (Kroll, Mintz Group, Control Risks), delivering comparable depth at a fraction of those firms' typical engagement minimums.

Can the report be used in employment proceedings?

The report is produced to evidential standard and is structured to inform employment decisions, including decisions to withdraw an offer based on findings. We strongly recommend involving employment counsel before any decision based on findings, particularly where adverse-media or behavioural signals are involved — Greyline produces the intelligence; legal advice on what to do with it is properly the role of an employment lawyer.

What if findings come back negative on a candidate I want to hire?

The Executive tier includes a 15-minute analyst briefing call specifically for this situation. Negative findings are not always disqualifying — context matters, and so does the candidate's opportunity to explain. The analyst can advise on whether further investigation is warranted, what questions you might put to the candidate, and how to think about the findings against the role.

Can I use this for foreign-national hires?

Yes. The Executive tier specifically includes country-of-origin criminal record checks and source-country field verification where available. Common origin countries handled regularly include Nigeria, Ghana, India, Pakistan, Philippines, Ukraine, Brazil, Colombia, and various Eastern European jurisdictions. Other countries are accommodated subject to attainability of records — the analyst will be transparent about what is achievable for each specific case.

Do you offer volume pricing for active hiring?

Yes. Clients commissioning five or more reports per month qualify for tiered volume discounts (10% at 5+, 15% at 10+, 20% at 25+). High-volume clients are usually placed on a retainer arrangement with a dedicated account manager and accelerated turnaround windows. Submit an inquiry to discuss retainer pricing for your hiring pipeline.

How is candidate information handled?

All candidate data is encrypted in transit and at rest, accessible only to the lead analyst and any specifically-authorised supporting personnel, retained in line with our Data Protection & Confidentiality Policy, and destroyed in accordance with stated retention periods. Candidates have full data subject rights including access, rectification, and erasure.
