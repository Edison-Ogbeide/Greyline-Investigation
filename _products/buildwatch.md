title: "BuildWatch: Overseas Construction Verification"
slug: buildwatch
description: "Independent, documented verification of construction progress on overseas property projects, with geotagged site records and title searches at outset."
standfirst: "Independent verification of what is actually happening on your building site, documented to an evidential standard, every month."
what_this_is:
  - "Owners funding construction from abroad rely on progress reports from the people spending the money. That arrangement works well when it is honest, and poorly when it is not."
  - "BuildWatch provides an independent record. A verified in-country associate attends the site on a scheduled monthly basis, working to a standardised documentation protocol agreed at the outset. Every report is reviewed and signed off by the principal before it is released."
  - "This is documented verification, not surveillance, and it is not an investigation of the contractor. It exists to give an owner a reliable, independent account of progress alongside whatever the contractor already reports."
included:
  - "Title and documentation search at the outset, checked against available public records"
  - "Scheduled monthly site attendance by a verified in-country associate"
  - "Geotagged, timestamped photographic and video record, captured to a fixed protocol"
  - "Materials observed on site, checked against the schedule provided by the client or contractor"
  - "A written monthly report, principal-reviewed, structured consistently for month-on-month comparison"
  - "A named point of contact for escalation between reports"
excluded:
  - "A valuation, quantity survey, or certification of the works"
  - "A legal opinion on title or ownership"
  - "Engineering, structural or building-standards inspection"
  - "Covert observation of any individual"
who_for:
  - "Property owners resident in the UK, United States, Canada or Australia funding construction in Nigeria most commonly instruct this work. Family offices and executors managing overseas estates also instruct it."
how_it_runs:
  - "An inquiry is submitted and a scoping call confirms the project, its location, and the schedule to check against."
  - "A title and documentation search establishes the position at the outset, against available public records."
  - "The in-country associate attends site on the agreed monthly date and records progress to the fixed protocol."
  - "The principal reviews the record against the schedule and prepares the monthly report."
  - "The report is delivered, with a named point of contact available for any question before the next attendance."
cadence:
  shape: "Ongoing"
  cadence: "Monthly"
  deliverable: "Written monthly report with a geotagged photographic and video record"
  review: "Principal-reviewed before release"
limitations_intro: "This work has defined boundaries, stated plainly so a client knows exactly what the record does, and does not, establish."
limitations:
  - "Title searches report what available records show at the date of search. They are not a legal opinion on title, and a qualified local solicitor should be instructed on any matter of ownership."
  - "Materials and progress are recorded as observed on the date of attendance, checked against the schedule the client or contractor provides. Greyline records observation; it does not certify the value or quality of the works."
  - "Site records are captured with the site owner's authority. Any identifiable third party appearing incidentally in the imagery is obscured before the record is released."
