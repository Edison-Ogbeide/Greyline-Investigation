FOR BUSINESSES

Intelligence for the
decisions that matter.

Hiring, exposure, insider risk, vendor due diligence, and the threats that don't appear in standard checks. Greyline supports UK SMEs, founders, executive teams, and family-owned businesses with intelligence that informs the decision before — not after — it goes wrong.

WHERE WE FIT

The intelligence layer
between data and decision.

Standard pre-hire screening confirms what a candidate has told you. Standard cybersecurity tests what your firewall blocks. Standard due diligence covers the documents.

Our work begins where those services end. We investigate the unstated — the digital pattern behind a CV, the supplier whose corporate filings hide a director conflict, the exposure of your executive team on the dark web, the breach response that needs more than a forensic report to actually repair.

Engagements are scoped, fixed-fee, and delivered as a written report. If we cannot defensibly close a piece of work, we say so before we start.

HOW WE ENGAGE

Three ways
to work with us.

Project Engagement

A defined-scope investigation with a fixed fee and delivery date. Best for one-off pre-hire reports, single dark-web assessments, vendor screening, or specific incident investigations. Most engagements deliver in 5–14 working days.

Retainer

A monthly retainer for clients with continuous needs — ongoing pre-hire screening volume, monthly exposure monitoring, vendor monitoring, or board-level intelligence. Includes priority response and capped-fee scoping for new work.

Incident Response

Same-day engagement for active incidents — suspected breach, executive impersonation, credible extortion threat, or live insider-threat suspicion. Initial scoping within 4 hours, on-site presence within 24 hours where required.
