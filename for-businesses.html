Hiring, exposure, insider risk, and the threats that don't appear in standard due diligence. Greyline supports UK SMEs, founders, executive teams, and family-owned businesses with intelligence that informs the decision before — not after — it goes wrong.

WHERE WE FIT

The intelligence layer
between data and decision.

Standard pre-hire screening confirms what a candidate has told you. Standard cybersecurity tests what your firewall blocks. Standard due diligence covers the documents.

Our work begins where those services end. We investigate the unstated — the digital pattern behind a CV, the credentials a senior hire forgot to mention, the exposure of your executive team on the dark web, the founder claim that doesn't quite hold up under cross-jurisdictional review.

Engagements are scoped, fixed-fee, and delivered as a written report. If we cannot defensibly close a piece of work, we say so before we start.

HOW WE ENGAGE

Three ways
to work with us.

Project Engagement

A defined-scope investigation with a fixed fee and delivery date. Best for one-off pre-hire reports, single dark-web assessments, or specific incident investigations. Most engagements deliver in 5–14 working days.

Retainer

A monthly retainer for clients with continuous needs — ongoing pre-hire screening volume, monthly exposure monitoring, or board-level intelligence. Includes priority response and capped-fee scoping for new work.

Incident Response

Same-day engagement for active incidents — suspected breach, executive impersonation, credible extortion threat, or live insider-threat suspicion. Initial scoping within 4 hours, on-site presence within 24 hours where required.
