Engagement Process

What happens after you instruct us.

Every engagement in this service line follows a clear process — whether productised or bespoke. Confidentiality is absolute, communication is via email and Google Meet only, and a single analyst remains your point of contact throughout.

Step One

Instruction

Productised services are instructed online via secure checkout. Bespoke engagements begin with a confidential discovery call, followed by a written scope and quotation.

Step Two

Intake

We send a secure intake form requesting the case-relevant information. All data is handled under our published Data Protection & Confidentiality Policy.

Step Three

Investigation

An analyst (and where applicable, field operatives) conducts the work. Productised services have defined turnaround windows; bespoke engagements progress against an agreed timeline.

Step Four

Delivery

You receive a written intelligence report — clear, structured, and actionable. For Continuous Intelligence, you receive ongoing alerts and digests across the engagement period.
