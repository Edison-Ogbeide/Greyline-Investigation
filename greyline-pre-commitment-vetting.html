The question this answers

"Before I marry, cohabit, or commit financially with this person — who are they really?"

The Greyline Pre-Commitment Vetting is the most comprehensive pre-commitment investigation we offer. It is built for the moment before a major life decision: marriage, cohabitation, joint property purchase, business partnership with a romantic partner, sponsorship of a partner's visa, or relocation to a new country to be with someone. These are the decisions that, if they go wrong, are catastrophic — financially, emotionally, and legally — and the cost of the investigation is a fraction of the cost of being wrong.

This product combines the entirety of the Greyline Digital Footprint Analysis (Tier 2) with documented public-record verification of identity, employment, and education; marital status indicators where lawfully accessible; link analysis mapping the subject's network of associates and contacts; timeline reconstruction; and an annotated, court-ready PDF evidence report. It concludes with a 60-minute debrief call with the lead analyst, where you receive the findings face-to-face and can ask the questions that matter most to you.

What is included

The complete scope.

  • Full Tier 2 Digital Footprint Analysis. Every element of the Tier 2 footprint analysis — social media audit, alias discovery, dating platform sweep, reverse image search, behaviour and posting analysis, network mapping, scored analytical report.
  • Identity cross-check against public records. The subject's claimed identity is verified against documented UK and international public records — electoral roll, Companies House, Companies Registry equivalents, and lawful jurisdiction-specific sources.
  • Employment and education verification. Claimed employment is verified through documented sources — direct verification with the employer where the subject's consent permits it, or through documented evidence of the role, the duration, and the subject's actual position.
  • Marital status indicators. Where lawful in the relevant jurisdiction, marital status, prior marriages, and divorce records are checked against the public record. Where these are not lawfully accessible (most non-UK jurisdictions), we explain the limitation in the report.
  • Link analysis. The subject's network of associates is mapped — known contacts, business associates, frequent interactions, and the structure of their public-facing relationships. Where the subject has presented themselves as isolated or unconnected, link analysis is often the first signal that the presentation is incomplete.
  • Timeline reconstruction. Major life events the subject has claimed (employment, education, residence, relationships, business activity) are placed on a timeline against documented public sources. Inconsistencies are surfaced and explained.
  • Annotated, court-ready PDF evidence report. Every finding is sourced, dated, and presented in a format suitable for civil proceedings — divorce petitions, prenuptial disputes, financial remedy applications, and matters where the credibility of the report itself will be tested.
  • 60-minute debrief call. Face-to-face debrief with the lead analyst on Google Meet. Findings presented, questions answered, recommendations explained.
