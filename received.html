How to recognise us

All correspondence comes from one place.

All Greyline communication relating to your case will come from an address ending in @greylineinvestigations.co.uk. We will never contact you by WhatsApp, SMS, social media DM, or phone call. A note on discretion

Read your delivery on a private device.

Your intake, your findings, and the fact of your engagement with Greyline are held in strict confidence. Our Data Protection & Confidentiality Policy sets out exactly how your information is stored, who can access it, and when it is destroyed. If you share a device, mailbox, or cloud account with anyone connected to this matter, we recommend reading your delivery on a device only you can access.
