What happens next

Your confirmation email contains everything.

A confirmation email has been issued to the address you nominated. It contains your case reference, the expected delivery timeline for your engagement, and any case-specific guidance that applies to your service.

You do not need to take any further action at this stage. If we require clarification on any element of your intake, the request will come from a Greyline analyst by email — referencing your case number — and only from an address ending in @greylineinvestigations.co.uk.

How to recognise us

All correspondence comes from one place.

All Greyline communication relating to your case will come from an address ending in @greylineinvestigations.co.uk. We will never contact you by WhatsApp, SMS, social media DM, or phone call. We will never ask you for your password, payment details, or any further fee beyond what you have already paid.

If you receive a message claiming to be from Greyline through any other channel, it is not us. Forward it to security@greylineinvestigations.co.uk and we will investigate.

A note on discretion

Read your delivery on a private device.

Your intake, your findings, and the fact of your engagement with Greyline are held in strict confidence. Our Data Protection & Confidentiality Policy sets out exactly how your information is stored, who can access it, and when it is destroyed. Findings are delivered once, to the email address you nominated, and are not retained on our systems beyond the period required by law.

If you share a device, mailbox, or cloud account with anyone connected to this matter, we recommend reading your delivery on a device only you can access.

If you need to reach us

Reply to your confirmation email.

For any matter relating to your case, reply directly to the confirmation email — quoting your case reference will help us route it quickly. For all other enquiries, contact cases@greylineinvestigations.co.uk.

We do not operate a telephone helpline. Where a call is necessary at any stage of your engagement, it will be conducted by Google Meet at a scheduled time, on a link issued from a Greyline address.
