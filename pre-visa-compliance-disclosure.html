SERVICE BOUNDARY · IMPORTANT

The Compliance Position of This Service

This service is commissioned by, and reports only to, the UK-based sponsor. It is a private personal due-diligence service directed at the integrity of a relationship, not at the immigration status of the partner.

  • Not an immigration screening service. This service does not assess, and does not produce findings on, the suitability or eligibility of any individual to enter, remain in, or be sponsored into the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, or any other jurisdiction.
  • Not for submission to immigration authorities. The output of this service is for the sponsor's personal records only. It is not admissible in, and must not be submitted to, UKVI, USCIS, IRCC, or any other immigration or border authority. Greyline will decline to participate in any such submission.
  • Subject is informed and consents. The polygraph element of this service requires the partner's express, informed, in-person consent. The OSINT element is conducted on publicly available information, in accordance with UK GDPR and our Data Protection & Confidentiality Policy.
  • We do not accept enforcement-side instruction. Greyline does not accept instruction from immigration authorities, immigration enforcement agencies, or sponsors seeking to substantiate the withdrawal of an existing sponsorship for enforcement purposes.
  • The boundary is the protection. This boundary exists to protect both the sponsor (whose due diligence is and must remain a private matter) and the partner (whose consent and dignity are preserved by the framing of the engagement).

If this framing does not match what you are looking for, this is not the appropriate service. We will be happy to refer you to qualified immigration solicitors who can advise on applicable visa-route requirements and lawful evidence preparation.
