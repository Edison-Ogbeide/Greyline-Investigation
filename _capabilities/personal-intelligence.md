Greyline's Personal Intelligence capability is quiet, documented inquiry into the people who matter most — partners, prospective spouses, those targeting you online, and those threatening your safety. This work is conducted lawfully and discreetly, on publicly available information and lawfully obtained records, with a named analyst accountable for every finding.
