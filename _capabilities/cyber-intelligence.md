Greyline's Cyber & Digital Risk Intelligence capability combines open-source intelligence, dark web exposure monitoring, adverse media screening and attack surface work into a single discipline. This is the digital trace work that underpins every other capability on this site — from vetting a candidate to tracing a fraud to preparing evidence for litigation — offered here as a standalone capability for CISOs, security teams and general counsel managing digital exposure directly.
