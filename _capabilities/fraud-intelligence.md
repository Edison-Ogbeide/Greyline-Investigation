Greyline's Fraud & Asset Intelligence capability investigates fraud, hidden assets and financial deception for individuals targeted by scams, businesses defending against loss, insurers pursuing subrogation, and litigators tracing assets before or after judgment. Work spans lifestyle evidence, source-of-wealth verification, and cryptocurrency tracing, with cross-border reach into the Nigeria / West Africa corridor through named in-country partners.
