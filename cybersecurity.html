How partner delivery works

Greyline scopes the engagement, selects the appropriate accredited delivery partner, manages the project end-to-end, and is the client's single point of accountability. The technical work is performed by tested specialist firms with the relevant accreditations (CREST, OSCP, CISSP and equivalents); the intelligence-led scoping, reporting standard, and client relationship sit with us. This protects clients from the cost and risk of unaccredited vendors, while ensuring that cybersecurity findings are integrated with the broader intelligence picture Greyline maintains for the client. Clients sign a single engagement agreement with Greyline; partner relationships are managed by us.

Engagement Process

From scoping call to remediation roadmap.

Cybersecurity engagements require disciplined scoping. Before a partner is selected and a quote is issued, we need to understand the environment, the threat profile, the regulatory context, and the outcome the client needs.

Step One

Discovery & Scoping

A confidential discovery call to understand environment, exposure, regulatory obligations, and desired outcomes. Where useful, technical pre-scoping with the client's IT or security lead.

Step Two

Partner Selection & Quote

Greyline selects the appropriate accredited delivery partner from our vetted bench. A written scope and combined quote is issued. Engagement terms are agreed under a single contract with Greyline.

Step Three

Authorised Execution

Work is conducted under signed rules of engagement, with Greyline's project management ensuring scope is respected, communication is structured, and the client has visibility throughout.

Step Four

Reporting & Roadmap

You receive an executive report and a technical findings report — with remediation prioritised against business risk. Where useful, retest engagements are scoped after remediation.
