This Cookie Policy explains how {{ site.company.legal_name }} uses cookies and similar technologies on this website. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites function correctly, improve performance, and provide information to website operators. Some cookies are essential for the website to work; others help us understand how visitors use the site.

2. Cookies We Use

2.1 Strictly necessary cookies. This website does not currently set any strictly necessary cookies. If that changes, this Policy will be updated to describe them.

2.2 Performance and analytics cookies. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site. It does not run, and no analytics cookie is set, unless you accept it through the cookie banner shown on your first visit. Consent is as easy to withdraw as to give: use the "Cookie Preferences" link in the footer of any page at any time to change your choice.

2.3 Third-party cookies. Other than Google Analytics, described above, this website does not use advertising, remarketing, or social media tracking cookies, and we do not permit third parties to set cookies through our website for their own purposes.

2.4 Third-party font delivery. This website loads its typefaces directly from Google Fonts. This request happens on every page load, regardless of your cookie choice, and may expose your IP address to Google as a normal function of that request. It does not set a cookie and is not linked to any Google account.

3. Server Logs

Our web server automatically records standard log data when you visit, IP address, browser type, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit. This data is used solely for security monitoring and aggregated traffic analysis, is not linked to any individual visitor, and is retained for no more than 13 months.

4. How to Manage Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies, consult your browser's help documentation for instructions. Disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect website functionality. For further information, visit www.allaboutcookies.org or www.youronlinechoices.com.

5. Legal Basis

Strictly necessary cookies are set under our legitimate interest in ensuring the website functions correctly, in accordance with Regulation 6 of the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR). We will obtain your explicit consent before setting any non-essential cookies, in compliance with PECR and UK GDPR.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page, and we will notify active clients of any material changes by email.
