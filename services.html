How Engagement Works

Two paths to begin.

Greyline operates two engagement models. Productised services are available for immediate instruction online — fixed scope, fixed price, defined turnaround. Bespoke engagements begin with a confidential discovery call to scope the work properly before any quote or commitment.

Path A — Productised

Choose your service

Select the productised service that matches your need — Greyline Verdict, Footprint Analysis, Pre-Commitment Vetting, Pre-Hire Intelligence Report, Continuous Intelligence, or Dark Web Sweep.

Path A — Productised

Instruct online

Complete payment securely. We confirm receipt, request the relevant case information through an encrypted intake form, and begin work immediately.

Path B — Bespoke

Submit an inquiry

Submit a confidential inquiry through the website. An analyst — not a salesperson — reviews your submission and books a 30-minute discovery call to scope the engagement.

Path B — Bespoke

Quoted and engaged

Following discovery, we issue a written scope and quotation. On agreement, terms are signed, the engagement begins, and a single analyst remains your point of contact throughout.
