Each engagement in this service line is scoped against the specific facts: who the actor is, where the money went, what jurisdictions are involved, and whether the priority is recovery, evidence for legal action, or both. Every engagement begins with a confidential discovery call.

Engagement Process

From discovery to deliverable.

Fraud and financial investigations require careful scoping. Before quoting, we need to understand the timeline of events, the parties involved, the financial flows, and the client's priority — recovery, evidence, both. Discovery calls are confidential and without obligation.

Step One

Discovery

A confidential 30-minute call. We understand what happened, what's known, what's needed, and confirm whether the engagement falls within our remit and lawful basis.

Step Two

Scope & Engagement

A written scope of work and quotation is issued. Engagement terms are agreed, payment is confirmed, and the engagement begins.

Step Three

Investigation

A lead analyst conducts the work — supported, where required, by crypto specialists, in-country operatives, or financial intelligence resources. Updates are provided at agreed intervals.

Step Four

Reporting & Onward Action

You receive an evidence-grade intelligence report — structured for use in civil claims, criminal referral, or recovery proceedings. We can liaise with your solicitors directly where helpful.

A note on lawful basis and recovery realism

Every engagement in this service line is conducted on a confirmed lawful basis under the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and the GDPR. We do not promise recovery — recovery depends on factors outside our control, including jurisdiction, asset location, and the willingness of foreign exchanges or authorities to act on findings. What we deliver is the intelligence that gives recovery the best possible chance, and the documentation that civil and criminal proceedings require.
