Every Greyline engagement draws on a defined set of intelligence disciplines, combined according to what the specific question requires:

- **OSINT**: open-source intelligence collection across public records, media, and the open web.
- **SOCMINT**: social media intelligence: platform-native analysis of public posts, networks and behaviour.
- **Cyber threat intelligence**: tracking threat-actor infrastructure, tooling and behaviour relevant to a client's exposure.
- **Entity resolution**: determining whether records, aliases or accounts across sources refer to the same real-world individual or organisation.
- **Identity intelligence**: verifying claimed identity against documentary and open-source evidence.
- **Behavioural analysis**: assessing patterns of activity, communication and lifestyle signal for consistency and anomaly.
- **Network and link analysis**: mapping relationships between people, entities and accounts to surface hidden connections.
- **Digital forensics**: forensically sound acquisition and analysis of digital evidence from devices and accounts.
- **Dark web intelligence**: structured, analyst-led investigation of dark web markets, forums and threat-actor channels.

Which disciplines apply to a given engagement is set out in the written scope, not assumed, a personal relationship verification draws on a different combination than a corporate insider-threat investigation, and the scope says which.
