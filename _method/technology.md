Greyline's analysts work with a defined stack of licensed OSINT, forensic and monitoring tooling, selected and maintained to the evidential standard described throughout this section, tools accelerate collection and cross-referencing, but every output is verified by a named analyst before it enters a report.

**Greyline Sentinel** is our internal platform for structured collection and case management, the system analysts use today to log sources, timestamp evidence, and maintain the chain-of-custody record described on the <a href="{{ '/method/evidential/' | relative_url }}">Evidential</a> page.

**Greyline Intelligence Cloud** is on our product roadmap, a future extension of Sentinel for clients who want ongoing, structured visibility into a live monitoring engagement. It is not yet built or available to instruct. We're naming it here so the direction of travel is transparent, not to describe a live capability or a plan a client can currently sign up to.
