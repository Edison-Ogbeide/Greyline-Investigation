Every piece of evidence Greyline collects is handled to a documented chain of custody from the moment it is captured. Original data is never altered, capture tools log hash values, timestamps and provenance automatically, and any subsequent handling is recorded in a contemporaneous log an independent third party could reproduce.

Where a deliverable may end up in front of a court, a family financial disclosure dispute, a defamation claim, a fraud recovery action, the same evidence standard applies from the first day of the engagement, not retrofitted once litigation becomes likely. Retrofitting evidential rigour after the fact is usually the point where a finding stops being defensible.

Handover to a client, solicitor, or court follows a documented process: what was collected, how, when, by whom, and what, if anything, has changed hands since. This is the standard against which every Greyline report is written, whatever capability it sits under.
