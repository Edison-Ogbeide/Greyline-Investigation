Pre-Investment Due Diligence Checklist.

Financial and legal due diligence answers whether the numbers and the contracts hold up. It rarely answers whether the people behind them, and the risks around them, do too. This checklist covers the intelligence gaps we most often find still open at the point a deal is meant to close.

{% include breadcrumb.html crumbs=page.crumbs %}

Leadership & Key Individuals

  • Undisclosed directorships, past or present, at connected or competing entities
  • Prior insolvencies, disqualifications, or regulatory sanctions against key individuals
  • Litigation history involving founders or executives, settled or ongoing
  • Discrepancies between claimed credentials and verifiable public record
  • Adverse media, in any jurisdiction the individual has operated in

Corporate Structure

  • Beneficial ownership traced through every intermediate holding entity
  • Related-party transactions not fully disclosed in the data room
  • Offshore entities in the ownership chain, and the reason for their use
  • Prior corporate names, mergers, or restructurings not reflected in current filings

Counterparties & Supply Chain

  • Sanctions and watchlist screening on major customers and suppliers, not just the target
  • Concentration risk in undisclosed related-party suppliers
  • Court proceedings or insolvency risk at key counterparties

Digital & Reputational Exposure

  • Data breach or credential exposure history for the target company
  • Unresolved cyber incidents not disclosed in warranties
  • Social media and public statements by key individuals that create reputational risk
