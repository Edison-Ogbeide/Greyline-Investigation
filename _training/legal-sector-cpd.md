Instructing digital and OSINT evidence well requires knowing what to ask for and what a defensible chain of custody actually looks like. This accredited session covers ACPO Principles for Digital Evidence, common pitfalls in instructing investigative work, and how to assess whether a report will hold up under cross-examination, delivered by the analyst who signs Greyline's own litigation-aligned reports.
