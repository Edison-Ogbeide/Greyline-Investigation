Boards are increasingly expected to demonstrate active oversight of cyber and fraud risk, not just sign off a report they didn't fully understand. This briefing gives trustees and C-suite the working literacy to ask the right questions of management and insurers, current threat landscape, what "good" looks like operationally, and the governance questions that actually matter, delivered directly by a named analyst, not a slide deck handed to HR.
