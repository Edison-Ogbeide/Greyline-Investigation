Boards are increasingly expected to demonstrate active oversight of cyber and fraud risk, not simply sign off a report they did not fully understand. Regulators, insurers and shareholders alike are asking sharper questions of governance in this area, and a board that cannot demonstrate genuine literacy is exposed regardless of how competent the underlying security team actually is. This briefing gives trustees and C-suite the working literacy to ask the right questions of management and insurers, covering the current threat landscape, what good practice looks like operationally, and the governance questions that actually matter, delivered directly by a named analyst rather than a generic slide deck.
