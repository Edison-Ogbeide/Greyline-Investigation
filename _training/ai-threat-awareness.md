The roles most exposed to AI-enhanced fraud, finance approving a payment, an executive assistant scheduling on a principal's behalf, are rarely the roles trained to spot it. Standard cyber awareness training covers phishing emails and suspicious links, but says little about a cloned voice on an urgent call, or a video meeting where the person on screen is not who they appear to be. This programme closes that specific gap, covering what AI-enhanced business email compromise, voice cloning and synthetic video calls actually look like in practice, and the verification habits that catch them, drawing on Greyline's own AI & Synthetic Intelligence casework rather than generic, off-the-shelf examples.
