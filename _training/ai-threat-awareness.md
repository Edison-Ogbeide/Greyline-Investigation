The roles most exposed to AI-enhanced fraud, finance approving a payment, an executive assistant scheduling on a principal's behalf, are rarely the roles trained to spot it. This programme covers what AI-enhanced business email compromise, voice cloning and synthetic video calls actually look like in practice, and the verification habits that catch them, drawing on Greyline's own AI & Synthetic Intelligence casework.
