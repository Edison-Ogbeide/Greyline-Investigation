Most breaches begin with an ordinary employee making an ordinary-looking decision: opening an attachment, reusing a password, approving a payment change over email. Generic compliance modules tend to focus on rules rather than the actual moment of decision, which is why they are so often completed and then forgotten. This programme is built around the judgement calls staff actually face, delivered by a named Greyline analyst, drawing on real, anonymised patterns from our own casework, and scoped to your sector and risk profile before delivery.
