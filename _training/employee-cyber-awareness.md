Most breaches begin with an ordinary employee making an ordinary-looking decision — opening an attachment, reusing a password, approving a payment change over email. This programme is built around the judgement calls staff actually face, not a generic compliance module. Delivered by a named Greyline analyst, drawing on real (anonymised) patterns from our own casework, and scoped to your sector before delivery.
