Awareness training without measurement is a compliance exercise. This programme runs simulated phishing and social engineering attempts against your organisation over an agreed period, measures how the organisation actually responds, not how it says it would, and follows up with targeted, non-punitive training for the patterns the simulation surfaces. Scoped and reported on by a named analyst throughout.
