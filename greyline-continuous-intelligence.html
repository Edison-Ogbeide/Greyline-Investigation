How This Service Is Different

The fear is the product.

Most monitoring products sell volume — alerts on everything, dashboards full of noise, raw automated outputs the client has to interpret. Greyline Continuous Intelligence is built around a different idea: tell us what you fear, and we watch specifically for it. Each engagement is structured around one or more concern profiles. Alerts are triggered only on confirmed findings relevant to the concern. Reports are written by an analyst, not generated by a script.

What You Receive

Ongoing intelligence, not endless noise.

You receive two types of output: scheduled intelligence digests on your tier's cadence, and real-time alerts on confirmed findings. Both are written by an analyst — not raw outputs from a tool — and structured to be acted on rather than catalogued.

Scheduled digest plus real-time alerts.

Each digest summarises what was monitored, what was found, what changed against the baseline, and what (if anything) requires attention. Where there is nothing to report, the digest says so plainly — silence is itself information.

Real-time alerts trigger only on confirmed findings: a hidden account discovered, a dating app reactivation detected, a contact-pattern anomaly flagged. We do not send raw automated alerts that the client has to triage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Things people need to know before subscribing.

Will the subject know they are being monitored?

No. The work is conducted entirely from open and lawful sources. The subject is not contacted, notified, or alerted at any stage of the subscription. Their devices are not accessed. Their private accounts are not entered. The monitoring is invisible to them.

Is this lawful in the UK?

Yes. Continuous Intelligence operates under the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and GDPR (UK) on confirmed lawful basis — typically the legitimate interests of a client protecting their household, family, or relationship. We document lawful basis at the start of each engagement.

Where the subject is a minor, we require parental authority (custody-confirmed or both-parent agreement). Where the subject is an adult third party, we confirm the lawful basis the client has for monitoring them — and decline engagements where no lawful basis exists.

What happens at onboarding?

Onboarding is a structured baseline exercise. We deep-baseline each subject across the chosen concern profiles, establish the digital footprint and behavioural pattern that constitutes "normal," and configure monitoring against that baseline. From there, deviations and meaningful changes are what trigger alerts and reports.

The £299 onboarding fee on monthly plans covers this baseline work; it is waived on annual prepay plans.

How quickly will I know if something significant is found?

Real-time alerts are triggered on confirmed findings — typically within hours of the finding being verified by an analyst. We do not send raw automated alerts; every alert has been reviewed by a human before reaching you.

Comprehensive tier includes priority incident response — a dedicated analyst available within an agreed window for urgent escalations.

Can I change concern profiles during the subscription?

Yes. Concerns can be adjusted month to month within the tier's profile limit. If a new concern emerges (a child receives a new phone, a partner shows new behaviours), you can swap or add concerns within your tier. Upgrading tier mid-subscription is supported with prorated billing.

Can I monitor someone without their knowledge?

The lawfulness of monitoring depends on context, jurisdiction, and the relationship between the client and the subject. We can monitor someone without their notification where the client has a clearly lawful basis (a parent monitoring a minor child, for example, or a person investigating their own household). For adult subjects in unclear contexts, we discuss lawful basis in detail before accepting the engagement.

Where lawful basis cannot be established, we decline. We do not undertake covert monitoring that crosses legal lines.

What if I want to cancel?

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled with 30 days' written notice. Annual prepay subscriptions run for the prepaid term and are not refundable for partial periods, but can be paused or transferred under our published terms.

On cancellation, all monitoring ceases immediately. Working files and reports are retained per our Data Protection & Confidentiality Policy retention schedule, then securely destroyed.

What if findings indicate something serious — like criminality?

Findings that indicate criminality (predator contact with a minor, evidence of fraud being committed against the client, etc.) are escalated immediately to the client with a clear recommended next-steps section. Where appropriate, we can liaise with law enforcement or specialist authorities at the client's instruction. Where the finding involves a vulnerable third party (e.g. a child being targeted), we advise on the appropriate reporting channels.
