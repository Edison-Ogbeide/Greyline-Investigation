The Question This Answers

"What if it changes? What if something happens I don't know about? How will I find out — before it's too late?"

One-time investigations answer the question of today. They cannot answer the question of next month, next quarter, or next year. The doubts that brought you to consider monitoring don't disappear because a single report came back clean — they reorganise around the next thing that might happen.

Continuous Intelligence exists for the people whose situation is ongoing. Whether you're watching a partner, protecting a child, or guarding an ageing parent from scam contact — the engagement is structured around what you actually fear, and we watch for it.

Concern Profiles

The fear is the product.

Every Continuous Intelligence engagement is built around one or more concern profiles — the specific thing the client wants watched. You choose the concern; we configure the monitoring infrastructure around it. Available concern profiles:

  • Infidelity WatchDating apps, hidden socials, suspicious communication patterns, location anomalies, behavioural drift consistent with a parallel relationship.
  • Adult Content WatchOnlyFans subscriptions, cam-site activity, escort directory contact, adult-platform consumption inconsistent with stated values.
  • Gambling WatchNew betting accounts, gambling app usage, financial signals of gambling, interactions with gambling communities.
  • Substance & Addiction WatchDrug-related online activity, supplier contact patterns, behavioural indicators of substance use or dependency.
  • Online Predator Watch (Minors)Grooming contact, sextortion attempts, dangerous community participation, age-inappropriate interactions targeting the minor subject.
  • Harmful Content Watch (Minors)Exposure to adult content, self-harm and eating-disorder content, radicalisation material, cyberbullying targeting the subject.
  • Romance Scam & Fraud WatchContact from known scam networks, unusual financial communications, suspicious romantic correspondence — typically commissioned for elderly relatives.
  • Hidden Identity WatchDiscovery of new aliases, secondary accounts, burner numbers, undisclosed online identities created during the monitoring period.
  • Reputation & Mention WatchPublic mentions, news, forum threads, court listings, or social media tags relating to the subject.
  • Behavioural Drift WatchBroad monitoring for any meaningful change in the subject's online behaviour from an established baseline.

What You Receive

Visibility, not noise.

Continuous Intelligence is built around the principle that automated alerts without analyst interpretation are worse than no alerts at all. Every alert you receive has been reviewed and contextualised by a human analyst before reaching you.

Output 01

Baseline Report

At engagement start, a complete footprint baseline establishing what is true today — against which all subsequent change is measured. Effectively a Tier 1 or Tier 2 Footprint, depending on tier.

Output 02

Real-Time Alerts

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions clients ask before subscribing.

Will the subject know they're being monitored?

No. Continuous Intelligence operates entirely through open-source intelligence and lawful data sources. The subject receives no notification, no contact, and no indication that any monitoring is taking place — at engagement start or during the engagement.

Is this lawful?

Yes — within strict limits. Greyline operates within the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and GDPR. We use only lawful sources of information: open-source intelligence, public records, breach data within the limits of lawful access, and information you as the client provide. We do not access private accounts, intercept communications, or use any unlawful means.

For monitoring of minors, parental authority provides the lawful basis. For monitoring of adult subjects, the engagement is configured to use sources that do not require subject consent.

How quickly do alerts arrive after a finding?

Confirmed findings are alerted within 24–48 hours of analyst review. Greyline does not push raw automated detections to clients — every alert is reviewed by an analyst first, contextualised, and only sent if it represents a meaningful finding rather than noise.

Can I add or change concern profiles after starting?

Yes. Concern profiles can be modified mid-engagement. Adding profiles within your tier's allocation is included; expanding beyond your tier's allocation requires upgrading to the next tier. The lead analyst can advise on the best approach based on your evolving situation.

What if I want to cancel?

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time, with no further charges from the next billing cycle. Annual prepay subscriptions are not refundable for the prepaid period, but cover the full year you've paid for.

If you cancel and return later, the engagement begins again with a fresh baseline — which protects the integrity of the monitoring against gaps.

What if nothing of concern happens?

You still receive intelligence digests at your tier's cadence, confirming that nothing of concern has occurred. This is genuinely valuable — many clients describe the absence of alerts as the second most reassuring outcome (after positive resolution of a specific concern). Continuous Intelligence is not designed to manufacture findings; it is designed to give you confidence whether or not findings exist.

Can I monitor a subject in another country?

Yes. International subjects are accommodated. Some countries with limited public records may produce shallower findings — the lead analyst will be transparent about what is achievable for your specific subject during onboarding.

How does this differ from Footprint Analysis?

Footprint Analysis is a one-time investigation that answers the question of today. Continuous Intelligence is an ongoing engagement that answers the question of every day going forward. Many clients commission a Footprint Analysis first to establish a baseline, and then convert into Continuous Intelligence to maintain visibility going forward.

How is my information handled?

All information is encrypted in transit and at rest, accessible only to the lead analyst and any specifically-authorised supporting personnel, and retained in line with our published Data Protection & Confidentiality Policy. Engagement records are retained for the duration of the subscription plus a defined retention period after cancellation; thereafter they are securely destroyed.
