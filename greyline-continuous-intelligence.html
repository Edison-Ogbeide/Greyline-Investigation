The question this answers

"I need to know what changes over time, not just a snapshot."

A point-in-time investigation answers the question of who someone was on the day the report was written. Greyline Continuous Intelligence answers the question of who they continue to be. It is a monthly subscription delivering sustained intelligence on a named subject — weekly monitoring, alert-driven flagging of material changes, and a monthly intelligence digest summarising what changed, what remained stable, and what warrants attention.

This product was built for the situations where one report isn't enough. A spouse with sustained concerns who needs to know if their partner re-creates a dating profile next month. A parent of a vulnerable adult whose online life keeps producing new exposure. A family member of a romance fraud victim, watching for signs that the victim is being re-targeted. A diaspora family wanting ongoing visibility into a relative's situation abroad. Continuous Intelligence sits in those gaps.

What is included

What the subscription actually delivers.

  • Weekly monitoring sweeps. Every seven days, the subject's known accounts, aliases, and platforms are re-checked. New activity is logged. Material changes are flagged.
  • New account and alias detection. Where the subject creates a new social media account, dating profile, or platform presence under a new handle, our discovery process flags it. Most clients are not concerned about Facebook posts — they are concerned about the new dating profile that wasn't there last month.
  • Dating platform activity-change flags. Re-activation, profile updates, new photographs, location changes, and "active in the last 24 hours" indicators are tracked across the major dating platforms in scope.
  • Monthly intelligence briefing. A structured monthly report (typically 6–10 pages) summarising the month: what changed, what remained stable, what warrants attention, and what — if anything — should be done about it.
  • Priority alert channel. Material findings (new dating profile, new alias account, named exposure event) trigger a real-time alert outside the monthly cadence.
  • Direct analyst access. A single named analyst owns the monitoring engagement. You have a priority email channel for questions, scope changes, or new concerns.
