The question this answers

"I need to know what changes over time, not just a snapshot."

A point-in-time investigation answers the question of who someone was on the day the report was written. Greyline Continuous Intelligence answers the question of who they continue to be. It is a monthly subscription delivering sustained intelligence on a named subject — continuous monitoring of their known accounts, platforms, and digital presence, with material changes flagged as they occur, and a comprehensive intelligence digest delivered at the end of every month.

This product was built for the situations where one report isn't enough. A spouse with sustained concerns who needs to know if their partner re-creates a dating profile next month. A parent of a vulnerable adult whose online life keeps producing new exposure. A family member of a romance fraud victim, watching for signs that the victim is being re-targeted. A diaspora family wanting ongoing visibility into a relative's situation abroad. Continuous Intelligence sits in those gaps.

What is included

The complete monitoring scope.

Every subscription is configured around a named monitoring focus. Clients select the watch category that matches their concern at outset — scope can be adjusted at any time via the direct analyst channel. Multiple categories can be combined.

Infidelity Watch

Active monitoring for behavioural indicators consistent with an undisclosed parallel relationship.

  • Dating application monitoring. Tinder, Bumble, Hinge, Ashley Madison, Seeking Arrangement, Grindr, Badoo, Plenty of Fish, and diaspora-specific platforms. New registrations, reactivated accounts, and residual traces of recent activity.
  • Hidden social account detection. Secondary Instagram, Snapchat, TikTok, and Facebook profiles not previously disclosed; alias registration across 300+ platforms.
  • Location and lifestyle anomaly signals. Strava activity, Google Maps reviews, TripAdvisor check-ins, and social media location data cross-referenced against stated whereabouts and routine.
  • Communication pattern shifts. Changes in posting cadence, device usage signals, and availability patterns that diverge from the established baseline.
  • Associate network changes. New significant relationships, unidentified recurring contacts, and social circle shifts not consistent with the subject's stated life.
Adult Content Watch

Monitoring for undisclosed engagement with paid adult platforms and escort services.

  • Subscription platform presence. OnlyFans, Fansly, Chaturbate, and equivalent subscription platforms — creator and subscriber activity where publicly indexed.
  • Escort directory and adult services monitoring. AdultWork, Vivastreet, and equivalent directories.
  • Adult lifestyle community monitoring. FetLife, Fab Swingers, Kasidie, and equivalent platforms.
  • Forum and community activity. Reddit communities, adult-oriented forums, and private group membership signals.
  • Financial distress cross-referencing. Where adult platform activity correlates with unexplained expenditure patterns visible in public financial signals.
Gambling Watch

Monitoring for undisclosed or escalating gambling behaviour and associated financial risk.

  • Betting account and gambling platform activity. Public-facing profiles, forum participation, and community signals across known gambling platforms.
  • Gambling community monitoring. Reddit (r/problemgambling and equivalent communities), self-exclusion references, and financial distress disclosures in public forums.
  • Financial distress indicators. New CCJs, unexplained credit activity signals, and insolvency markers cross-referenced with gambling activity patterns.
  • Behavioural escalation signals. Posting frequency, language patterns, and community engagement consistent with problem gambling behaviour.
Hidden Identity Watch

Monitoring for undisclosed secondary identities, aliases, and covert online presence.

  • Alias and username discovery. Continuous sweep of 300+ platforms for new registrations linked to the subject's known identifiers, photographs, or digital signatures.
  • Reverse image monitoring. Subject's photographs monitored for reuse across unconnected profiles, identity substitution, or AI-generated image deployment.
  • Burner account and secondary social profile detection. Signals of secondary phone numbers, email addresses, and platform accounts not previously disclosed.
  • Cross-platform identity consistency. Monitoring for divergence between the subject's stated identity and the digital pattern of activity across all known platforms.
Behavioural Drift Watch

Broad-spectrum monitoring for any meaningful change from the subject's established digital baseline. Recommended for clients who want comprehensive coverage without committing to a specific concern category at outset.

  • Full digital footprint change detection. Any material shift across social media, forums, marketplaces, professional platforms, and community participation.
  • Lifestyle and location pattern changes. Divergence from established routines, travel patterns, and stated circumstances.
  • Associate and network shifts. Changes in the composition or character of the subject's social circle.
  • Analyst-led pattern assessment. Cumulative changes reviewed holistically to identify emerging risk patterns that individual alerts would not surface in isolation.
Reputation & Mention Watch

Proactive monitoring so families and individuals can respond to public mentions before they escalate.

  • News archive and media monitoring. Structured alerts for subject mentions in national and regional news, court reporting, and investigative journalism.
  • Forum and community mention detection. Reddit, community forums, complaints databases, and review platforms (Glassdoor, Trustpilot, Google Reviews) monitored for subject references.
  • Court listing monitoring. Civil and criminal court listings cross-referenced against the subject's known identifiers.
  • Social media tagging and mention signals. Public tags, mentions, and references across Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok, and LinkedIn.
  • Analyst triage. Every mention assessed for reputational risk level before alert is issued. Clients are not notified of irrelevant noise.
  • Recommended response window. Where a reputationally sensitive mention is detected, the analyst provides a recommended response timeline and options.
Online Predator Watch

Monitoring for grooming contact, exploitation attempts, and dangerous relationships involving minors.

  • Grooming contact pattern detection. Monitoring for contact from adult accounts exhibiting grooming indicators: age-inappropriate relationship building, requests for secrecy, escalating personal requests, and gift or reward offers.
  • Sextortion attempt monitoring. Detection of coercive contact patterns consistent with sextortion methodology, including unsolicited explicit content, identity-based threats, and financial demands.
  • Dangerous community participation. Monitoring for the minor's involvement in communities or platforms known to facilitate exploitation, predatory contact, or child sexual abuse material distribution.
  • Age-inappropriate platform detection. Alerting where the minor is active on platforms restricted to adults or known for unmoderated adult contact.
Harmful Content Watch

Monitoring for the minor's exposure to content categories associated with psychological harm.

  • Self-harm and eating disorder content exposure. Community participation, content consumption signals, and social connections in communities promoting self-harm, anorexia, bulimia, or related content.
  • Radicalisation and extremist content monitoring. Exposure to far-right, far-left, incel, or jihadist content communities; escalating engagement signals.
  • Adult content exposure. Platforms and communities serving explicit material to underage users.
  • Cyberbullying detection. Monitoring for public instances of targeted harassment, humiliation campaigns, pile-ons, or coordinated exclusion involving the minor as target or participant.
  • Analyst advisory. All findings in this module are delivered with sensitivity to the minor's welfare. Recommended next steps focus on safeguarding outcomes, not punitive response.
Monthly Intelligence Digest
  • End-of-month intelligence report. A structured monthly report (typically 6–10 pages) delivered at the close of each subscription month. The report summarises what changed across the monitoring period, what remained stable, what warrants attention, and — where relevant — what action is recommended. Every finding is sourced and dated.
  • Direct analyst access. A single named analyst owns the monitoring engagement. You have a priority email channel for questions, scope changes, or new concerns between monthly digests.
  • Quarterly behaviour analysis. Every three months, the accumulated monitoring data is reviewed for longer-term behavioural patterns — shifts in lifestyle, communication changes, and emerging signals that the monthly snapshot alone may not surface.
Optional Add-On · +£100/month

Real-Time Alert
When waiting until month-end is not an option.

The monthly digest tells you what happened. The Real-Time Alert tells you when it is happening.

Standard monitoring is structured around the monthly cycle — findings accumulate, patterns are assessed, and the digest lands at month-end. That cadence works for most situations. But some findings cannot wait four weeks. An active affair does not pause while your report is being assembled. A scam in progress does not slow down because the monitoring period hasn't closed. A teenager in contact with a predator needs a response today, not on the first of next month.

The Real-Time Alert retainer places a senior Greyline analyst on standby for exactly those moments. When monitoring detects a material finding — a new dating profile, a re-activated account, a new alias, a named exposure event — you receive an alert within hours, not weeks. And if that alert requires action, your analyst is ready to move immediately.

What the Real-Time Alert delivers

  • Immediate material finding alerts. New dating profile detected, dormant account re-activated, new alias account created, dark web exposure confirmed — you receive a direct alert within 4 hours during business hours. No waiting for month-end.
  • Analyst-led incident scoping. When an alert fires, your named analyst contacts you directly to scope what has been found, what it means, and what — if anything — should be done about it immediately.
  • Rapid deep-dive on confirmed incidents. Where a material finding requires investigation beyond the standard monitoring scope — tracing a new alias, mapping a contact network, or documenting an active deception — the analyst moves immediately, rather than queuing the work for the next cycle.
  • Court-ready evidence packaging. Where the finding has legal implications — infidelity proceedings, financial remedy applications, child protection matters, or fraud — the incident documentation is prepared in a format suitable for use in civil or family court.
  • Recommended action protocol and trusted referrals. Where the situation requires more than intelligence — a solicitor, a family liaison specialist, a financial investigator — your analyst provides a vetted referral from Greyline's trusted network.

The Real-Time Alert add-on is available at subscription start or can be activated at any time during an active subscription. Contact your analyst or submit an inquiry to add it.
