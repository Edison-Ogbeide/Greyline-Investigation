Litigation & Legal Support

Investigative support for solicitors and family offices.

Greyline supports legal teams with the factual and investigative work that sits underneath strong cases. We deliver evidence to standards that survive disclosure and cross-examination, document our methodology, and structure our reports to feed directly into pleadings, witness statements, and expert reports. Engagements are scoped under privilege where appropriate, and our analysts are available for instruction as expert witnesses where the matter requires.

Investigative Support for Civil Litigation

Pre-action and ongoing investigative support for civil litigation — fact-finding, witness location, document trace, asset visibility, and the structured intelligence work that strengthens claim merits and informs settlement strategy. Reports are delivered in a format that supports pleadings, witness statements, and disclosure obligations under Civil Procedure Rules.

Quoted post-inquiry
Suited for: solicitors preparing civil claims, in-house counsel scoping pre-action investigation, family offices supporting litigation matters, and litigators needing factual reinforcement before instruction.

Expert Witness & Court Reporting

Expert reports prepared to Civil Procedure Rules Part 35 and Criminal Procedure Rules standards — for digital evidence, OSINT findings, fraud reconstruction, asset tracing conclusions, and other matters within Greyline's competence. Reports are structured for court submission, the analyst is available for cross-examination where instructed, and prior testimony experience is disclosed in advance.

Quoted post-inquiry
Suited for: solicitors needing expert evidence in fraud, digital, or relationship-related civil matters, criminal defence and prosecution teams, and family law practitioners requiring digital evidence reports.

Witness Location & Verification

Locate witnesses, verify identity, confirm current address, and document availability — for solicitors preparing for trial, mediation, or pre-action correspondence. Conducted lawfully under UK and target-jurisdiction rules, with chain of custody preserved on any evidence collected during the trace.

Quoted post-inquiry
Suited for: solicitors preparing for trial or disclosure, claimants seeking to establish witness availability, and matters where a witness has gone non-responsive or moved without forwarding details.
