Litigation &
Legal Support

Intelligence and evidence support for solicitors, barristers, and litigants — work structured to be deployable in proceedings, not just informative on a desk. Documentation prepared to evidential standard, chain-of-custody maintained from the outset, and a single analyst available to your matter team across the engagement lifecycle.

Bespoke Engagements

Built for matter teams.

Litigation engagements are scoped against the matter, the proceedings, and the standard of evidence required. Whether the engagement is a single discrete instruction or ongoing support across a complex matter, the work is structured to be admissible, defensible, and immediately usable by counsel.

Working with solicitors and counsel

Greyline routinely works alongside instructing solicitors as part of a matter team. We accept instructions on a Letter of Engagement basis, can be retained directly or via the firm, and are comfortable working under instructions that preserve litigation privilege. Where helpful, we attend matter conferences via Google Meet, brief counsel directly on findings, and produce reports in formats specified by the instructing firm. Our work supports — rather than replaces — the legal strategy set by counsel.

Engagement Process

How matter engagements begin.

Litigation engagements begin with a confidential scoping call between the instructing solicitor (or counsel) and Greyline's lead analyst. Discovery calls are confidential and conducted under terms appropriate to the matter sensitivity.

Step One

Scoping Call

A confidential call between the instructing party and Greyline's analyst to understand the matter, the intelligence question, the standard of evidence required, and any sensitivities.

Step Two

Letter of Engagement

A written scope, fee structure, and engagement letter is issued. Privilege-preserving instructions are accommodated where the instructing firm requires.

Step Three

Investigation

A lead analyst conducts the work. Updates are provided at agreed intervals. Field operatives, in-country agents, and specialist subcontractors are engaged where required, under analyst supervision.

Step Four

Deliverable

A structured intelligence report, evidence pack, witness statement, or hybrid output as specified — produced to evidential standard and ready for use in proceedings.
