How This Differs

Not every matter needs to be scoped from scratch.

Most Greyline work is scoped individually against the specific facts of a matter. This section covers the exception: recurring or fixed-scope engagements where the question is already well understood and the deliverable is standardised.

Every one of these engagements is still principal-reviewed and produced to the same evidential standard as bespoke work.
